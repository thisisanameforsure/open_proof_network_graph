$$\forall M\in\mathbb{N}\ \exists N\ge M:\ \Big\{\sum_{n=2}^{N}\frac{N!}{n!-1}\Big\}\le 1-\frac{1}{N}$$

Here $\{\cdot\}$ is the fractional part and the sum is over $2\le n\le N$.
