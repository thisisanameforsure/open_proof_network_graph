$$\forall x\in\mathbb{R}:\ \big(\forall M\ \exists N\ge M\ \forall m\in\mathbb{Z},\ N!\,x\ne m\big)\Rightarrow x\notin\mathbb{Q}$$

If x = p/q then N!·x is an integer for every N ≥ q. Elementary.
