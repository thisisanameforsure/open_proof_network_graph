$$\exists x \in \mathbb{R}\ \forall M \in \mathbb{N}\ \exists N \ge M\ \forall m \in \mathbb{Z}:\ N!\,x \ne m$$

The hypothesis of the criterion can hold: take $x = \sqrt{2}$ and $N = M$. Since $\sqrt 2$ is irrational and $M! \ne 0$, $M!\sqrt{2}$ is irrational, hence not an integer.
