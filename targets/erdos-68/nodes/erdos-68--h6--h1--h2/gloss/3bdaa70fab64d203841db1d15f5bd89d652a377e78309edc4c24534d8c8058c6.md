$$\forall M\ \exists N\ge M:\ \tfrac{2}{N+1}\le\{N!\,S\}<1-\tfrac{1}{N+1},\quad S=\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{1}{n!-1}$$

Equivalently, the digit $a_{N+1}=\lfloor (N+1)\{N!\,S\}\rfloor$ of $S$ in base factorial satisfies $2\le a_{N+1}\le N-1$.
