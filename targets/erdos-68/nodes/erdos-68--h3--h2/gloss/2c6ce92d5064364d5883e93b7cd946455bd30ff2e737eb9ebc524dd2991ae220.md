$$\exists \delta>0\ \forall M\ \exists N\ge M:\ \delta\le\operatorname{frac}\Big(N!\sum_{n\ge 2}\frac{1}{n!-1}\Big)\le 1-\delta$$

In words: the distance from N!·S to the nearest integer does not tend to 0.
