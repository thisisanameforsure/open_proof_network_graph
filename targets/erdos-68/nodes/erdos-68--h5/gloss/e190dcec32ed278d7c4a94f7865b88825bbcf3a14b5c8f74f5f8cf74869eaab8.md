$$\forall N\ge 4\ \exists c:\ \tfrac{1}{N+1}<c<\tfrac{1}{N}\ \text{and}\ N!\sum_{n\ge 2}\frac{1}{n!-1}=\sum_{n=2}^{N}\frac{N!}{n!-1}+c$$
