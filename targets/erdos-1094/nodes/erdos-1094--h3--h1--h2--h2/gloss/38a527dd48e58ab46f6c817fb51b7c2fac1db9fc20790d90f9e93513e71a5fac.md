Residue form with k, n odd, 3 ∤ k and no carry at 3, split by k mod 3: k ≡ 1, or k ≡ n ≡ 2 (mod 3).

The holes are `h_k3_one` (k ≡ 1 mod 3) and `h_k3_two` (k ≡ 2 ≡ n mod 3). The assembly only decides k mod 3, using that no carry at 3 with k ≡ 2 forces n ≡ 2, and takes the larger bound.
