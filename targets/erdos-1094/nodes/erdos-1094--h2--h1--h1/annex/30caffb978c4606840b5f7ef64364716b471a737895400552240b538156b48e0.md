Structure forced by the hypotheses (B, t1014-y, 2026-10-14). The divisibility claim below is proved in Lean in the assemblies of PRs #514, #515, #516; the remarks after it are mine and NOT machine-checked.

Fact (proved in Lean). Let g = gcd(n,k). From k*C(n,k) = n*C(n-1,k-1) and gcd(n/g, k/g) = 1, n/g divides C(n,k). Hence minFac C(n,k) <= minFac(n/g) whenever n/g >= 2.

Consequences.
1. On --h2--h1--h1 (2k <= n < k^3, minFac C(n,k) > k): every prime factor of n/gcd(n,k) exceeds k (PR #516's hole carries this as a hypothesis). Unchecked remark: if moreover k | n, then n/k < k^2 has all prime factors > k, so n/k is a prime in (k, k^2); in general n = g*m with g | k and m a product of at most two primes above k (since m < k^3/g).
2. On --h2--h1--h4 and --h2--h2--h3 (k^2 <= n, minFac C(n,k) > n/k): n/gcd(n,k) is prime (PR #514, #515). So n = g*p with g | k, g < k and p a prime > n/k. Unchecked remark: then floor(n/k) = floor(g*p/k), and the hypothesis k*floor(n/k) | k! says floor(n/k) has all prime factors < k.

Suggested next step for a successor (unchecked): on the holes, split on g = gcd(n,k). For g = 1 (n coprime to k) the hole is about primes-free windows; for g > 1 write n = g*p and apply Kummer's theorem prime by prime for p' <= k (no carries when adding k and n-k in base p').
