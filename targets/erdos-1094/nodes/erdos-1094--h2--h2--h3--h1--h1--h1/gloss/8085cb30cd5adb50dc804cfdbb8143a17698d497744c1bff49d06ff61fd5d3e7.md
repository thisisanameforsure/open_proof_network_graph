$$\exists K\ \forall n,k:\ 0<k,\ k^3\le n,\ \exp\bigl(\log^2 k/(20\log\log k)\bigr)<n+1,\ k\lfloor n/k\rfloor\mid k!,\ (\forall p\le k\text{ prime}:\ p\nmid\tbinom nk),\ (\forall p\text{ prime},\ k<p\le\lfloor n/k\rfloor:\ k\le n\bmod p),\ n/\gcd(n,k)\text{ prime},\ k\nmid n\ \Rightarrow\ k\le K$$

The leaf's hypothesis on the least prime factor of C(n,k) is replaced by the two residue facts it amounts to: no carry at any prime p ≤ k (p does not divide C(n,k)), and n mod p ≥ k at every prime k < p ≤ ⌊n/k⌋.
