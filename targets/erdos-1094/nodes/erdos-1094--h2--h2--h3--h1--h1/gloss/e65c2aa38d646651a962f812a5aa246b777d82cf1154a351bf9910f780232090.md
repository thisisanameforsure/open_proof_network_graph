The leaf's hypothesis $\operatorname{minFac}\binom nk>\lfloor n/k\rfloor$ is replaced by the two residue facts it amounts to, and the leaf is left in that residue form as the one hole.

**What is proved here.** Write $B=\lfloor n/k\rfloor$; from $k^3\le n$ we have $k\le B$.

1. *Primes $p\le k$.* Such a $p$ is at most $B$, so $p\nmid\binom nk$ directly from the hypothesis on the least prime factor.
2. *Primes $k<p\le B$.* If $n\bmod p<k$, then $p$ divides $n-(n\bmod p)$, which is one of the $k$ factors $n,n-1,\dots,n-k+1$ of $n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)=k!\binom nk$; since $p>k$ is prime it does not divide $k!$, so $p\mid\binom nk$, against $p\le B<\operatorname{minFac}\binom nk$. Hence $n\bmod p\ge k$: no multiple of $p$ lies among $n-k+1,\dots,n$.

**The hole.** `h_residue`: $0<k$, $k^3\le n$, $\exp(\log^2k/(20\log\log k))<n+1$, $k\lfloor n/k\rfloor\mid k!$, no prime $p\le k$ divides $\binom nk$, $n\bmod p\ge k$ for every prime $k<p\le\lfloor n/k\rfloor$, $n/\gcd(n,k)$ prime and $k\nmid n$ imply $k\le K$. Its node is `erdos-1094--h2--h2--h3--h1--h1--h1`. The two residue facts are together equivalent to the hypothesis they replace, so the hole is the leaf itself in the form in which the analytic attacks read it; the hypothesis at primes $p\le k$ can be sharpened further to Kummer's digit condition $k\bmod p^i\le n\bmod p^i$ for all $i$ (a short Lean derivation from `Nat.Prime.emultiplicity_choose` is in the annex on the hole).

**The assembly.** Given the hole's $K$, apply it to $(n,k)$ with the two facts above supplied.
