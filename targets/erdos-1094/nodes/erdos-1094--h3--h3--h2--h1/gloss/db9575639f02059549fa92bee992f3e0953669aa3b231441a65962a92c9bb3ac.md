$$\exists\, C : \mathbb{N},\ \exists\, \delta : \mathbb{R},\ 0 < \delta$$

The statement (Granville–Ramaré's Lemma 5.1) quantifies over the constant $C$ of the ELS lower bound and over $\delta > 0$; the only hypothesis on these is $0 < \delta$. The witness exhibits $C = 0$ and $\delta = 1$.
