There is a natural number K such that, for all natural numbers n and k with k positive and n at least k squared, if the least prime factor of the binomial coefficient C(n, k) is greater than the floor of n/k, then k is at most K.

In other words, the pairs (n, k) with n at least k squared whose binomial coefficient has no prime factor at most the floor of n/k all have k bounded by one constant. Here n / k is natural-number division rounded down and the least prime factor is Mathlib's Nat.minFac.
