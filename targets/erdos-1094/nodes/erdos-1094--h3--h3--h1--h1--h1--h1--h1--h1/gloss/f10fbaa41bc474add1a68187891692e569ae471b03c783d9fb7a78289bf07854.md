$$\exists C,\ \forall N,\ \sum_{n<N} f(n) \le C\,N$$

One C² bump with ψ ≥ 1 on [1/4, 1] and support in (0, 2) bounds each block Σ_{N/4<n≤N} f(n) by (A∫ψ + 1)N for large N; summing the blocks over N, N/4, N/16, … gives the linear bound.
