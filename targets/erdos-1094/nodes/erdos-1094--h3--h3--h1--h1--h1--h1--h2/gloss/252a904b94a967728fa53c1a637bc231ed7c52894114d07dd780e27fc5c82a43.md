$$\forall \psi \in C_c^\infty(0,\infty):\ \tfrac{1}{X}\sum_{n} f(n)\,\psi(n/X) \to A\int_0^\infty \psi \text{ as } X \to \infty$$

The Tauberian core: with L(f, s) − A/(s−1) continuous on Re s ≥ 1, every C² test function supported in (0, ∞) sees the mean A, by Mellin inversion and the Riemann–Lebesgue lemma.
