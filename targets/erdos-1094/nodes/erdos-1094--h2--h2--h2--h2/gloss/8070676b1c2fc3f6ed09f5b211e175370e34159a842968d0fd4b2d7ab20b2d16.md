Konyagin's count on the medium range: few $m$ in $(k, k + k^\theta)$ have $n/m$ near an integer, so BHP's primes leave one that divides $(n-k)\cdots(n-1)$.

The one hole is `h_count`, the bound $3 + 56k^{2\theta-1} + k^\theta(\log k)^{-2}$ on the $m$ in $(k, k + k^\theta)$ with $n/m$ within $k^{\theta-1}$ of an integer, for $k^{2-\theta}/2 < n \le k^2/\log^2 k$ and $\theta = 21/40$. The assembly proves that a window prime with $n/p$ far from every integer divides $\prod(n - i)$ (`dvd_from_far`), that the bound is eventually below $C k^\theta/\log k$ (`med_rhs_le`), and closes by pigeonhole against BHP's prime count (`konyagin_finish`).
