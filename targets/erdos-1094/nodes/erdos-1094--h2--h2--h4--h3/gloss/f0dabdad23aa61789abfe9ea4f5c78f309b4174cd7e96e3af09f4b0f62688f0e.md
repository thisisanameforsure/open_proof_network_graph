$$\exists\, \eta \in \mathbb{R},\ 0 < \eta$$

The witness exhibits a positive real number, because the only hypothesis of the statement that precedes its existential quantifier is $0 < \eta$: the statement reads $\forall \eta > 0,\ \exists c > 0,\ \exists k_0,\ \ldots$, and everything from $\exists c$ on belongs to the conclusion. The Lean witness is $\eta = 1$ with `one_pos`.

It says only that the statement is not vacuous in $\eta$; nothing about the sets $S$ of near-rational points it bounds.
