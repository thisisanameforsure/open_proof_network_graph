$$\forall \eta > 0\ \exists c > 0\ \exists k_0\ \forall k \ge k_0\ \forall n,\ k^3 \le n \le \exp(c \log^2 k):\ \#\{m \in (k, k + k^{3/5}]: \exists a \in \mathbb{Z},\ 1 \le q \le k^{1/100},\ |n/m - a/q| < k^{3/5}/k\} < \eta k^{3/5}$$

Here $m$, $q$, $n$, $k$ are natural numbers and the inequalities are between real numbers. The Lean says it for every finite set $S$ of such $m$: $S$ has fewer than $\eta k^{3/5}$ elements.
