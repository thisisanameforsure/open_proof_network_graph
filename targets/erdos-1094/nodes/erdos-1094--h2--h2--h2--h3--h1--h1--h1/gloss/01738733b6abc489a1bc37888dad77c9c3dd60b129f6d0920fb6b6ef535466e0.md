Prove Konyagin's theorem at scale $[1, 2)$ from one lemma: a small integer linear relation killing the first $r$ moments of $2r$ weighted points, with coefficients at most $(256M/r)^{r-1}\cdot W$.

The one hole is `hsilr`. The assembly proves the short-interval bound (`short_interval`: fewer than $2r$ good points in any block of length $M$, since the relation makes a nonzero integer smaller than $1$), counts blocks for $\lambda = 1$ (`block_subdivision_count`, `lambda_one_case`), and compares the constant $C_0$ with $65536$ at a scale $\lambda'$ in $[1, 2)$ (`algebraic_comparison`).
