Residue form with k, n odd and 3 | k, split at the prime 5: either 5 | k too, or 5 ∤ k and no carry at 5 (a carry at 5 is closed by p = 5).

The holes are `h_k15` (k ≡ 0 mod 3 and mod 5) and `h_k3_rest5` (5 ∤ k with k mod 5 ≤ n mod 5), both with k and n odd. The assembly takes the larger bound with k ≥ 5 and answers n mod 5 < k mod 5 with p = 5.
