$$\exists K\ \forall n,k:\ 0<k,\ k^3\le n,\ \exp\bigl(\log^2 k/(20\log\log k)\bigr)<n+1,\ k\lfloor n/k\rfloor\mid k!,\ (\forall p\le k\text{ prime},\ \forall i:\ k\bmod p^i\le n\bmod p^i),\ (\forall p\text{ prime},\ k<p\le\lfloor n/k\rfloor:\ k\le n\bmod p),\ n/\gcd(n,k)\text{ prime},\ k\nmid n\ \Rightarrow\ k\le K$$

The residue form with, at every prime p ≤ k, each base-p digit of k at most the corresponding digit of n (no carry adding k and n−k; equivalently the window n−k+1..n holds exactly ⌊k/p^i⌋ multiples of p^i for every i), and n mod p ≥ k at every prime k < p ≤ ⌊n/k⌋.
