$$\exists c > 0\ \exists k_0\ \forall k \ge k_0\ \forall n,\ k^3 \le n \le \exp(c \log^2 k):\ \exists p \text{ prime},\ p \le k,\ p \mid \tbinom{n}{k}$$

Here $\log$ is the natural logarithm, $n$ and $k$ are natural numbers, and the upper bound on $n$ is an inequality between real numbers.
