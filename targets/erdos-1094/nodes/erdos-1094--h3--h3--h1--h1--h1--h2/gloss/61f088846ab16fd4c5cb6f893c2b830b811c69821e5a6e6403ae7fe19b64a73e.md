$$\sum_n \Lambda(n) n^{-s} - \frac{1}{s-1}$ extends continuously to $\Re s\ge 1$$

From ζ's simple pole of residue 1 at s = 1 and ζ(s) ≠ 0 on Re s ≥ 1 (Mathlib: riemannZeta_residue_one, riemannZeta_ne_zero_of_one_le_re, ArithmeticFunction.LSeries_vonMangoldt_eq_deriv_riemannZeta_div).
