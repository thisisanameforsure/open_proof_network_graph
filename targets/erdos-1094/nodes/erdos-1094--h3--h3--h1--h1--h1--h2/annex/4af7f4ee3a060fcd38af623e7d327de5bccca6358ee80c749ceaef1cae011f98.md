Informal argument for the pole lemma (the hole `-ζ'/ζ minus the pole extends to Re s ≥ 1`).

Let $\Lambda$ be the von Mangoldt function. For $\operatorname{Re} s > 1$ the Dirichlet series $L(\Lambda, s) = \sum_n \Lambda(n) n^{-s}$ converges and equals $-\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$. The Riemann zeta function has a simple pole at $s = 1$ with residue $1$ and no zero on the closed half-plane $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 1$ (the non-vanishing on the line $\operatorname{Re} s = 1$ is the de la Vallée Poussin / Hadamard input). Hence $-\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s) - 1/(s-1)$ is holomorphic on $\operatorname{Re} s > 1$ and extends continuously to $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 1$: at $s = 1$ the pole of $-\zeta'/\zeta$ (simple, residue $1$) is cancelled, and elsewhere on the line both $\zeta$ and $\zeta'$ are continuous with $\zeta \ne 0$. That extension is the $G$ the statement asks for.

In Mathlib this is already packaged, in the generality of a residue class $a \bmod q$ (used for Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions):

```
ArithmeticFunction.vonMangoldt.LFunctionResidueClassAux (a : ZMod q) : ℂ → ℂ
continuousOn_LFunctionResidueClassAux : ContinuousOn (LFunctionResidueClassAux a) {s | 1 ≤ s.re}
eqOn_LFunctionResidueClassAux (ha : IsUnit a) :
  Set.EqOn (LFunctionResidueClassAux a)
    (fun s ↦ LSeries (residueClass a ·) s - (q.totient : ℂ)⁻¹ / (s - 1)) {s | 1 < s.re}
```

Take $q = 1$ and $a = 1$ (the only element of `ZMod 1`, a unit). Then every $n$ lies in the residue class, so `residueClass 1 n = Λ n` for all $n$ (the set `{n | (n : ZMod 1) = 1}` is everything, by `Subsingleton`), and $\varphi(1) = 1$. The two lemmas are then exactly the continuity and the identity the hole states, with $G$ := `LFunctionResidueClassAux (1 : ZMod 1)`. No analytic estimate has to be redone: the whole content is Mathlib's non-vanishing of $\zeta$ on $\operatorname{Re} s = 1$ and the residue at $1$.
