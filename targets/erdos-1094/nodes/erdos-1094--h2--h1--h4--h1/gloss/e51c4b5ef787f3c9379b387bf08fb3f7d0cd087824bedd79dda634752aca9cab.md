On the thin strip with n/gcd(n,k) prime, split by gcd(n,k): either n itself is prime and coprime to k, or gcd(n,k) ≥ 2.

The holes are `h_cop` (n prime and gcd(n,k) = 1) and `h_div` (gcd(n,k) ≥ 2), both carrying the strip hypotheses k² ≤ n < k(k + 3k^(21/40)), k⌊n/k⌋ ∣ k! and lpf C(n,k) > n/k. The assembly shows gcd(n,k) < 2 forces gcd(n,k) = 1 and n = n/gcd(n,k) prime, and takes the larger bound.
