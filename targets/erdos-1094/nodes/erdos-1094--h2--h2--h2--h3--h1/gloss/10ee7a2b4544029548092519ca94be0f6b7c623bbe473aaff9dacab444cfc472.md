Reduce the count of $m$ with $n/m$ near an integer to Konyagin's theorem on the good points of a smooth $f$, applied to $f(x) = (-1)^r n/(k + x)$ on $[0, k^\theta]$ with $W = 1$.

The one hole is `h_thm`, Konyagin's theorem: a bound on the integers $u$ in $[0, N]$ at which a $C^{r+1}$ function $f$ with $|f^{(r)}|$ between $D_r/2$ and $D_r$ lies within $\delta$ of a rational with denominator at most $W$. The assembly builds a smooth extension of $n/(k + x)$ (`smoothDenom`, `fK`), computes its derivative bounds (`abs_iteratedDeriv_r`, `abs_iteratedDeriv_r1`), shows each bad $m$ is a good point (`isGood_fK`) and reads off the stated bound (`konyagin_application`).
