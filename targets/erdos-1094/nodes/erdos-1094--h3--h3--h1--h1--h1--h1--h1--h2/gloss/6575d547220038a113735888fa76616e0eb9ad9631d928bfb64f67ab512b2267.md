$$\bigl(\exists C,\ \forall N,\ \sum_{n<N} f(n) \le C N\bigr) \Rightarrow \tfrac{1}{N}\sum_{n<N} f(n) \to A$$

Given the linear bound, for ε > 0 take δ small and bumps ψ₋ ≤ 1_{[δ,1]} ≤ ψ₊ supported in (δ/2, 1+δ) with ∫ψ₊ − ∫ψ₋ ≤ ε; the smoothed limits for ψ± and the bound Σ_{n<δN} f(n) ≤ CδN give limsup and liminf of S(N)/N within O(ε + δ) of A.
