There is a natural number K such that, for all natural numbers n and k with k positive, n at least 2k and n less than k squared, if the least prime factor of the binomial coefficient C(n, k) is greater than k, then k is at most K.

In other words, the binomial coefficients C(n, k) with 2k at most n and n below k squared whose prime factors all exceed k occur only for k bounded by one constant. The least prime factor is Mathlib's Nat.minFac.
