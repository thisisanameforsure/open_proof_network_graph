The holes are `h_k3one` (k ≡ 1 mod 3 with 1 ≤ n mod 3) and `h_k3two` (k ≡ 2 ≡ n mod 3), each carrying the no-carry hypotheses at 5, 7, 11 and 13. The assembly only decides k mod 3 and takes the larger bound.
