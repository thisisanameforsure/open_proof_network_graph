$$\text{BHP fails, or } \exists k_0\ \forall k \ge k_0\ \forall n,\ 2k \le n \le \tfrac12 (k/2)^{2-21/40} + 2k:\ \exists p \text{ prime},\ k < 2p,\ (n \bmod p) + p < k$$

The condition $(n \bmod p) + p < k$ says that two multiples of $p$ lie among $n-k+1, \dots, n$. BHP is the statement of the hole `h_bhp`.
