$$f\ge0$, $\sum f(n)n^{-s}$ converges for $\Re s>1$ and $F(s)-A/(s-1)$ extends continuously to $\Re s\ge1$ $\Rightarrow$ $\frac1N\sum_{n<N}f(n)\to A$$

Wiener (1932), Ikehara (1931); continuous-boundary form: Korevaar, Bull. AMS 39 (2002), Thm 4.2 / Tauberian Theory (2004) III.4.
