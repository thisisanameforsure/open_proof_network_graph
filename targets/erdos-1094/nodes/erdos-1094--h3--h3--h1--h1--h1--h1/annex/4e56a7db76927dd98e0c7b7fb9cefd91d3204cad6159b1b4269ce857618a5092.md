Informal argument for the Wiener–Ikehara skeleton merged as PR #1449 (two holes).

**Setting.** $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ is non-negative, $F(s) = \sum_n f(n) n^{-s}$ converges for $\operatorname{Re} s > 1$, and $F(s) - A/(s-1)$ extends continuously to $\operatorname{Re} s \ge 1$. Claim: $S(N)/N \to A$ where $S(N) = \sum_{n < N} f(n)$.

**Step 1 (`h_smooth`, the Tauberian core).** For every $\psi \in C^2_c(0,\infty)$ (twice continuously differentiable, compact support inside $(0,\infty)$),
$$\frac{1}{X}\sum_{n} f(n)\,\psi(n/X) \longrightarrow A\int_0^\infty \psi(x)\,dx \qquad (X \to \infty).$$
This is the smoothed form of the theorem, the one Mellin inversion proves: $\sum_n f(n)\psi(n/X) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{(\sigma)} F(s)\,\tilde\psi(s)\,X^{s}\,ds$ with $\tilde\psi$ the Mellin transform of $\psi$, which decays like $|t|^{-2}$ on vertical lines because $\psi \in C^2$ with compact support; move the contour to $\sigma = 1$ using the continuous extension of $F(s) - A/(s-1)$, pick up $A\,\tilde\psi(1) X = A X\int\psi$ from the pole, and the remaining integral is $o(X)$ by the Riemann–Lebesgue lemma. PrimeNumberTheoremAnd (Kontorovich, Tao et al., `Wiener.lean`) carries this out in Lean 4 for smooth $\psi$; its proof applies verbatim to $C^2$ test functions, since $C^2$ is what the decay of $\tilde\psi$ needs. The $C^2$ hypothesis (rather than $C^\infty$) is deliberate: the gate's hole printer cannot write `⊤` smoothness back as Lean (it prints `↑⊤` with no source type), and $C^2$ is both weaker to assume here and enough for Step 2.

**Step 2 (`h_unsmooth`, unsmoothing with $f \ge 0$).** Assume Step 1 for every such $\psi$.

(a) *Linear bound.* Take one $C^2$ bump $\psi$ with $\psi \ge 1$ on $[1/4, 1]$ and support in $(0, 2)$ (Mathlib's `ContDiffBump` centred at $1$ with radii $3/4 < 1$). Non-negativity of $f$ gives, for $N$ large, $S(N) - S(N/4) \le \sum_n f(n)\psi(n/N) \le (A\int\psi + 1)N =: cN$. Summing over the blocks $N, N/4, N/16, \dots$ gives $S(N) \le \tfrac{4}{3}cN + O(1)$, so $S(N) \le CN$ for all $N$.

(b) *Sandwich.* Fix $\varepsilon > 0$ and a small $\delta > 0$. Choose $C^2$ bumps $\psi_- \le \mathbf 1_{[\delta,1]} \le \psi_+$ with $\operatorname{supp}\psi_+ \subset (\delta/2, 1+\delta)$, $\psi_- = 1$ on $[2\delta, 1-\delta]$, and $\int\psi_+ - \int\psi_- \le 6\delta$ (again `ContDiffBump`, whose integral lies between twice the inner and twice the outer radius). Then
$$\frac{1}{N}\sum_n f(n)\psi_-(n/N) \;\le\; \frac{S(N) - S(\delta N)}{N} \;\le\; \frac{1}{N}\sum_n f(n)\psi_+(n/N),$$
and $0 \le S(\delta N)/N \le C\delta$ by (a). Letting $N \to \infty$ with Step 1 for $\psi_\pm$, the liminf and limsup of $S(N)/N$ lie within $A\cdot 6\delta + C\delta$ of $A$; $\delta \to 0$ gives the limit.

The skeleton's assembly is `exact h_unsmooth h_smooth`. Step 2 is elementary real analysis and is the hole to take first; Step 1 is the port.
