$$\exists\, c \in \mathbb{R},\ 0 < c$$

The witness for this node exhibits a positive real constant, because the statement's only outer hypothesis is $0 < c$: it reads $\forall c > 0,\ \exists K,\ \forall n, k, \ldots$, and the quantifier over $K$ and everything after it belongs to the conclusion, not to the hypotheses a witness must satisfy. The Lean witness is $c = 1$ with `one_pos`.

So the witness says only that the statement is not vacuous in $c$; it says nothing about whether any pair $(n, k)$ satisfies the inner hypotheses (that is the content of the node, and of the open problem).
