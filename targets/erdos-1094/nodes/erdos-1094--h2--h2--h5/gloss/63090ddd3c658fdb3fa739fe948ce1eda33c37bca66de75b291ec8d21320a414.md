$$\forall c > 0\ \exists K\ \forall n, k:\ 0 < k,\ k^3 \le n,\ \exp(c \log^2 k) < n,\ \operatorname{lpf}\tbinom{n}{k} > \lfloor n/k \rfloor \implies k \le K$$

Here $\operatorname{lpf}$ is the least prime factor (`Nat.minFac`), $n / k$ is floor division, and $\exp(c \log^2 k) < n$ is an inequality between real numbers.
