## Overview

The node asks, for every $c > 0$, for a bound $K$ such that $k \le K$ whenever $0 < k$, $k^3 \le n$, $\exp(c\log^2 k) < n$ and $\operatorname{lpf}\binom{n}{k} > \lfloor n/k \rfloor$. The partial takes two holes and proves the assembly: the first hole, `h_prod`, is the identity $\prod_{m=n-k+1}^{n} s_k(m) = k!\cdot s_k\binom{n}{k}$ for $0 < k \le n$, where $s_k(x) = \prod_{p \le k \text{ prime}} p^{v_p(x)}$ is the $k$-smooth part; the second, `h_core`, is the node's own question with two more hypotheses: no integer of the window $(n-k, n]$ has a prime factor in $(k, \lfloor n/k \rfloor]$, and $\prod_{m=n-k+1}^{n} s_k(m) = k!$. The assembly derives both extra hypotheses from the node's and hands them to `h_core`.

**The smooth parts multiply to k!.**

From $k^3 \le n$ and $k \ge 1$ we get $k \le n$ and $k \le \lfloor n/k \rfloor$ (as $k \cdot k \le n$). No prime $p \le k$ divides $\binom{n}{k}$: if it did, `Nat.minFac_le_of_dvd` would give $\operatorname{lpf}\binom{n}{k} \le p \le k \le \lfloor n/k \rfloor$ against the hypothesis. So every exponent $v_p\binom{n}{k}$ with $p \le k$ is zero (`Nat.factorization_eq_zero_of_not_dvd`), the product $s_k\binom{n}{k}$ is $1$ (`Finset.prod_eq_one`), and `h_prod` reads $\prod_{m} s_k(m) = k!$.

**The sieve step.**

Let $p$ be a prime with $k < p \le \lfloor n/k \rfloor$ dividing some $m$ with $n - k < m \le n$. Then $n - m < k$, so $m = n - (n-m)$ is a factor of the descending factorial $n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1) = \prod_{i<k}(n-i)$ (`Nat.descFactorial_eq_prod_range`, `Finset.dvd_prod_of_mem`), which equals $k!\binom{n}{k}$ (`Nat.descFactorial_eq_factorial_mul_choose`). As $p > k$, $p \nmid k!$ (`Nat.Prime.dvd_factorial`), so $p \mid \binom{n}{k}$ (`Nat.Prime.dvd_mul`), and then $\operatorname{lpf}\binom{n}{k} \le p \le \lfloor n/k \rfloor$, contradicting the hypothesis. Hence the window is free of such primes, which is the first extra hypothesis of `h_core`; with the identity it yields $k \le K$.
