$$\forall n,k:\ 0<k\le n,\ \bigl(\forall p \text{ prime},\ k<p\le\lfloor n/k\rfloor,\ \forall m\in(n-k,n],\ p\nmid m\bigr) \Rightarrow \forall m\in(n-k,n]:\ s_k(m)=m\ \text{ or }\ s_k(m)<k$$

Here $s_k(m) = \prod_{p \le k \text{ prime}} p^{v_p(m)}$ is the $k$-smooth part of $m$, with $v_p(m)$ the exponent of $p$ in $m$ (`Nat.factorization m p`).
