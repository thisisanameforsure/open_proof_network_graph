$$\exists\, C : \mathbb{N},\ \exists\, \varepsilon : \mathbb{R},\ 0 < \varepsilon$$

The statement quantifies over the constant $C$ of the ELS lower bound $k^2 \le C\, n \log_2 k$ and over $\varepsilon > 0$ in the $o(k)$ conclusion; the only hypothesis on these is $0 < \varepsilon$. The witness exhibits $C = 0$ and $\varepsilon = 1$.
