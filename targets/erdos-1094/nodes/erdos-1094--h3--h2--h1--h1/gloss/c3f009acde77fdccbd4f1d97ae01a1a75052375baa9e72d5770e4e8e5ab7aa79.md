$$\exists c>0\,\exists K\,\forall n,k:\ K<k,\ 2k\le n<k^2,\ \mathrm{lpf}\binom{n}{k}>k \Rightarrow k^{1+c}<n$$

The Ecklund–Erdős–Selfridge bound restricted to the only range where it has content.
