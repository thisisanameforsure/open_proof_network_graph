If $k\mid n$ the hypotheses are contradictory for $k\ge 2$, so the leaf keeps only the case $k\nmid n$.

**What is proved here.** Suppose $k\mid n$. Then $\gcd(n,k)=k$, so the hypothesis that $n/\gcd(n,k)$ is prime says $p:=n/k$ is prime. The hypothesis $k\cdot\lfloor n/k\rfloor\mid k!$ gives $p\mid k!$, so $p\le k$ because $p$ is prime. But $k^3\le n$ gives $p=n/k\ge k^2$, and $k^2>k$ for $k\ge2$: a contradiction. For $k=1$ the bound $k\le\max(K,1)$ holds outright, which is why the assembled bound is $\max(K,1)$ rather than $K$.

**The hole.** `h_ndvd`: the parent's hypotheses ($0<k$, $k^3\le n$, $\exp(\log^2k/(20\log\log k))<n+1$, $k\lfloor n/k\rfloor\mid k!$, $\operatorname{minFac}\binom nk>\lfloor n/k\rfloor$, $n/\gcd(n,k)$ prime) together with $k\nmid n$ imply $k\le K$. Its node is `erdos-1094--h2--h2--h3--h1--h1`, the open core of the target beyond van Doorn–Tang's range: there $n=gP$ with $P$ prime and $g=\gcd(n,k)$ a proper divisor of $k$.

**The assembly.** Case on $k\mid n$: the impossible case above, or the hole's bound.
