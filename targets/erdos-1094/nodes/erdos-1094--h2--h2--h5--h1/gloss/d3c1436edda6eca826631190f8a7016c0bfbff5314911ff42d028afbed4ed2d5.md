$$\forall n,k:\ 0<k\le n \Rightarrow \prod_{m=n-k+1}^{n}\ \prod_{p\le k,\ p \text{ prime}} p^{v_p(m)} = k!\cdot\prod_{p\le k,\ p \text{ prime}} p^{v_p\binom nk}$$

Here $v_p(m)$ is the exponent of $p$ in $m$ (`Nat.factorization m p`); the products over primes $p \le k$ are the $k$-smooth parts of $m$ and of $\binom nk$.
