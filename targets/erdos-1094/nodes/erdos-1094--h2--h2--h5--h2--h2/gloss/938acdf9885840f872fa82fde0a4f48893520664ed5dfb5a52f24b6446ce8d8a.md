$$\forall c>0\ \exists K\ \forall n,k:\ 0<k,\ k^3\le n,\ \exp(c\log^2 k)<n,\ \operatorname{lpf}\binom nk>\lfloor n/k\rfloor,\ \bigl(\forall p \text{ prime},\ k<p\le\lfloor n/k\rfloor,\ \forall m\in(n-k,n],\ p\nmid m\bigr),\ \prod_{m=n-k+1}^{n} s_k(m) = k!,\ \bigl(\forall m\in(n-k,n]:\ s_k(m)=m \text{ or } s_k(m)<k\bigr) \Rightarrow k\le K$$

Here $s_k(m) = \prod_{p \le k \text{ prime}} p^{v_p(m)}$ is the $k$-smooth part of $m$. The open core with the window's arithmetic shape in hand.
