Residue form with k even, split at the prime 3: either 3 | k, or 3 ∤ k and no carry at 3 (a carry at 3 is closed by p = 3).

The holes are `h_k6` (k ≡ 0 mod 2 and mod 3) and `h_k3rest` (3 ∤ k with k mod 3 ≤ n mod 3). The assembly takes the larger bound with k ≥ 3 and answers n mod 3 < k mod 3 with p = 3.
