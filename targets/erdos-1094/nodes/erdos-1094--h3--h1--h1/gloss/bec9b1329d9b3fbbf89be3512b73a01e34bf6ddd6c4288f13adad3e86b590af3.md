There is a natural number K₁ such that, for all natural numbers n and k with k greater than K₁, k even, n at least 2k and n less than k squared, there is a prime p at most k for which the remainder of n on division by p is smaller than the remainder of k on division by p.

Here n % p and k % p are natural-number remainders. Nothing is said about odd k, and nothing about C(n, k) itself.
