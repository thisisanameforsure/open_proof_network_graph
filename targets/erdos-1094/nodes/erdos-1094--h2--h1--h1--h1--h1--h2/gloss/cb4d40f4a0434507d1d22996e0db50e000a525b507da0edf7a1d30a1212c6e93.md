$$\exists K\ \forall n,k,q:\ 2k\le n<k^2,\ n=\gcd(n,k)\,q,\ q>k \text{ prime},\ \gcd(n,k)\ge2,\ \operatorname{minFac}\binom nk>k \Rightarrow k\le K$$
