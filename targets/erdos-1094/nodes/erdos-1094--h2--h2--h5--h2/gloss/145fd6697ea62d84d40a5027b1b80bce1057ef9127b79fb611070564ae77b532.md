$$\forall c>0\ \exists K\ \forall n,k:\ 0<k,\ k^3\le n,\ \exp(c\log^2 k)<n,\ \operatorname{lpf}\binom nk>\lfloor n/k\rfloor,\ \bigl(\forall p \text{ prime},\ k<p\le\lfloor n/k\rfloor,\ \forall m\in(n-k,n],\ p\nmid m\bigr),\ \prod_{m=n-k+1}^{n}\prod_{p\le k \text{ prime}} p^{v_p(m)} = k! \Rightarrow k\le K$$

The sieve-free case: no integer among n-k+1, ..., n has a prime factor in (k, n/k], and their k-smooth parts multiply to k!.
