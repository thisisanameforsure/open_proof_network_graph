Large range via Konyagin's theorem with $r$ the least exponent with $n\cdot r! \le k^{r+\theta}$ and $\lambda$ balanced: the bad $m$ are $o(k^\theta/\log k)$, so BHP leaves a prime dividing the window.

The one hole is `h_kon`, the same Konyagin bound as on the medium-large range. The assembly takes the least $r$ with $n\cdot r! \le k^{r+\theta}$, near $\log k/(10 \log\log k)$ (`large_r_data`), chooses $\lambda = \mathrm{lamLarge}$ so that the first two terms equal $k^{(\theta-1)/(3r-2)}$ (`large_card_raw`), bounds the result by $C k^\theta/\log k$ for large $k$ (`large_asym`), and closes by pigeonhole (`case_large`).
