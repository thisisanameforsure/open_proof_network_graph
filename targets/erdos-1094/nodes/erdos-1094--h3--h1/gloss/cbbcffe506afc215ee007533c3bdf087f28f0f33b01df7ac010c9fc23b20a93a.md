There is a natural number K such that, for all natural numbers n and k with k greater than K, n at least 2k and n less than k squared, there is a prime p at most k for which the remainder of n on division by p is smaller than the remainder of k on division by p.

Here n % p and k % p are natural-number remainders. Nothing is said about C(n, k) itself; the statement is purely about remainders modulo primes up to k.
