For every positive natural number k, only finitely many natural numbers n satisfy both: n is at least k squared, and the least prime factor of the binomial coefficient C(n, k) is greater than the floor of n/k.Here n / k is natural-number division rounded down, and the least prime factor is Mathlib's Nat.minFac. The finite set may depend on k; nothing is said about how its size grows with k.
