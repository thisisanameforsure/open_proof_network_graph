$$\psi(x) = x + o(x)$, i.e. $\forall \varepsilon>0\ \exists X\ \forall x\ge X,\ |\psi(x)-x|\le\varepsilon x$$

The prime number theorem in Chebyshev's ψ form: Hadamard (1896), de la Vallée Poussin (1896); e.g. Ingham, The Distribution of Prime Numbers (1932), Thm 3 / Apostol, Intro. to Analytic NT, Thm 4.4 + §13.
