Medium-large range via Konyagin's theorem at $r = 2$ and scale $\lambda = \sqrt{k^{2+\theta}/2n}\cdot\log k$: the bad $m$ are $o(k^\theta/\log k)$, so BHP leaves a prime dividing the window.

The one hole is `h_kon`, Konyagin's bound on the $m$ in $(k, k + k^\theta)$ with $n/m$ within $k^{\theta-1}$ of an integer, for any $\lambda \ge 1$ and $2 \le r \le k^{1-\theta}/2$. The assembly instantiates it with $r = 2$ and $\lambda = \mathrm{lamML}$ (`ml_card_bound`), shows the resulting bound $g_{\mathrm{ml}}$ is eventually below $C k^\theta/\log k$ (`ml_rhs_le`), and closes by pigeonhole against BHP's prime count (`konyagin_finish`).
