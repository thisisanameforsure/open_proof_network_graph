## Overview

The node asks for a bound $K$ with: for all $n, k$ with $0 < k$, $k^3 \le n$ and $\operatorname{lpf}\binom{n}{k} > \lfloor n/k \rfloor$, we have $k \le K$. The partial splits the range $n \ge k^3$ at $n = \exp(c \log^2 k)$ for a constant $c > 0$ that the first hole supplies, and proves the assembly as the private lemma `KR_final`: given the two holes, take $K = \max(k_0, K')$ where $k_0$ comes from the first hole and $K'$ from the second hole at that same $c$. Suppose $k > K$, so $k \ge k_0$ and $k > K'$. Either $n \le \exp(c \log^2 k)$, and the first hole gives a prime $p \le k$ dividing $\binom{n}{k}$; then $\operatorname{lpf}\binom{n}{k} \le p \le k \le \lfloor n/k \rfloor$ (the last step because $k \cdot k \le n$, from $k^3 \le n$ and $k \ge 1$), contradicting the hypothesis. Or $n > \exp(c \log^2 k)$, and the second hole gives $k \le K'$, contradicting $k > K'$. The two cases are exhaustive, so $k \le K$.

## The Konyagin range {steps: s1.h_kfull}

The first hole states: there are $c > 0$ and $k_0$ such that for every $k \ge k_0$ and every $n$ with $k^3 \le n \le \exp(c \log^2 k)$ (an inequality of real numbers, with $\log$ the natural logarithm), some prime $p \le k$ divides $\binom{n}{k}$. In the assembly it is used only through $\operatorname{lpf}\binom{n}{k} \le p$, which `Nat.minFac_le_of_dvd` gives from $2 \le p$ and $p \mid \binom{n}{k}$, together with $p \le k \le \lfloor n/k \rfloor$. Nothing in the file proves the hole; its label names Konyagin's range.

## Beyond the Konyagin range {steps: s2.h_edge}

The second hole states: for every $c > 0$ there is $K'$ such that for all $n, k$ with $0 < k$, $k^3 \le n$, $\exp(c \log^2 k) < n$ and $\operatorname{lpf}\binom{n}{k} > \lfloor n/k \rfloor$, we have $k \le K'$. The assembly instantiates it at the constant $c$ the first hole produced, and applies it in the case $n > \exp(c \log^2 k)$, which is the negation of the first case's bound. The hole is the node's own question restricted to $n$ above $\exp(c \log^2 k)$; again nothing in the file proves it.
