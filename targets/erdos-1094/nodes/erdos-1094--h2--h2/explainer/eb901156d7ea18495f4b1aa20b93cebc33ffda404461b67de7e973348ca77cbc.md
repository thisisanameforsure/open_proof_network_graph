## The argument

The theorem's body applies one lemma, `KR_final`, to the two holes.

**The two inputs.** (`h_kfull`) There are $c > 0$ and $k_0$ such that for every $k \ge k_0$ and every $n$ with $k^3 \le n \le \exp(c\log^2 k)$ some prime $p \le k$ divides $\binom nk$. (`h_edge`) For every $c > 0$ there is $K$ such that every pair with $0 < k$, $k^3 \le n$, $\exp(c\log^2 k) < n$ and least prime factor of $\binom nk$ above $\lfloor n/k \rfloor$ has $k \le K$.

**Assembly** (`KR_final`). Take $c$ and $k_0$ from `h_kfull` and then $K$ from `h_edge` at this $c$. Let $0 < k$, $k^3 \le n$ and the least prime factor of $\binom nk$ exceed $\lfloor n/k\rfloor$; we show $k \le \max(k_0, K)$. Suppose $k > \max(k_0, K)$. If $n \le \exp(c\log^2 k)$, `h_kfull` gives a prime $p \le k$ dividing $\binom nk$; since $k^2 \le k^3 \le n$ we have $k \le \lfloor n/k \rfloor$, so the least prime factor is at most $p \le k \le \lfloor n/k\rfloor$, a contradiction. Otherwise $\exp(c\log^2 k) < n$ and `h_edge` gives $k \le K$, again a contradiction.
