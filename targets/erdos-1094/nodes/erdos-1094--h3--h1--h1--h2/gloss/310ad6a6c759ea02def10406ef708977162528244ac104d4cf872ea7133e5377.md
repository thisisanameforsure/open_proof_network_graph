Residue form with k even, 3 ∤ k and no carry at 3: try the primes 5, 7, 11, 13 in turn, and leave as the hole the case where none of them carries.

The one hole is `h_p5to13`, the same statement with k mod p ≤ n mod p for p = 5, 7, 11, 13 added. The assembly requires k ≥ 13 and, if n mod p < k mod p for any of those four primes, answers with that prime.
