$$\forall C > 0\ \exists k_0\ \forall k \ge k_0:\ \#\{m \in (k, k + k^\theta): \|n/m\| < k^{\theta-1}\} < C k^\theta/\log k \text{ for } k^2/\log^2 k < n \le \tfrac12 k^{2+\theta},\ \theta = 21/40$$

Here $\|x\|$ is the distance from $x$ to the nearest integer and $m$ runs over integers.
