label: Left projection from a conjunction

$$\forall\, p\,q:\operatorname{Prop},\quad p\wedge q\ \Rightarrow\ p$$

For any two propositions $p$ and $q$, if $p$ and $q$ both hold then $p$ holds. There are no other hypotheses.
