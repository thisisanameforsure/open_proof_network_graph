label: Summability of the prime sum 1/(2^p - 1)
---
$$\sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{p}-1} < \infty$$

The family $p \mapsto 1/(2^p - 1)$, indexed by the primes and valued in $\mathbb{R}$, is summable in Mathlib's sense (unconditionally; for a nonnegative real family this is ordinary convergence).
