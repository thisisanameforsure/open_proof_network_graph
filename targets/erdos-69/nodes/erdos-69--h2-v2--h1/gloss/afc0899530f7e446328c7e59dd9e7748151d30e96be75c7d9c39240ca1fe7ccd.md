label: Some rescaled tail times b is not an integer (untyped casts)

$$\Bigl(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\uparrow\omega(n)}{2^{n}} = \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{\uparrow p}-1}\Bigr) \Rightarrow \forall b\in\mathbb{N},\ b>0\ \ \exists N\in\mathbb{N}\ \ \forall z\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \uparrow b\cdot\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\uparrow\omega(N+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} \ne \uparrow z$$

If the series of $\omega(n)/2^n$ equals the sum over primes of $1/(2^p - 1)$, then for every $b \ge 1$ there is an $N$ such that $b$ times the tail $\sum_{j\ge 0} \omega(N+1+j)/2^{j+1}$ is no integer $z$. Every cast arrow $\uparrow$ in the Lean carries no target type, so as written neither the hypothesis nor the tail is stated over $\mathbb{R}$, where the parent's hole states them: their domain is whatever Lean's defaults assign ($\mathbb{N}$ for a bare numeral, where division rounds down and $2^p - 1$ is truncated subtraction), and $\sum'$ is the tsum (0 when not summable).
