label: Irrationality of the level sets of omega in base 2
---
$$\forall m\in\mathbb{N},\ m\ge 1:\quad \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\bigl[\omega(n)=m\bigr]\Bigl(\frac{1}{2}\Bigr)^{n} \notin\mathbb{Q}$$

For every natural $m \ge 1$, the real number with a binary 1 in place $n$ exactly when $n$ has $m$ distinct prime factors, the sum over $n \ge 0$ of $(1/2)^n$ when $\omega(n) = m$ and 0 otherwise, is irrational. The series is a tsum over $\mathbb{R}$ (0 when not summable), and `Irrational x` means $x$ is not the cast of a rational.
