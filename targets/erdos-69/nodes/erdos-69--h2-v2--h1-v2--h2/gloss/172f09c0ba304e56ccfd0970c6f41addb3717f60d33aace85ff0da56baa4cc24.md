label: Positivity of the rescaled tail
---
$$\forall M\in\mathbb{N}:\quad 0 < \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(M+1+j)}{2^{j+1}}$$

Under the hypotheses that the Lambert identity $\sum_{n\ge 0} \omega(n)/2^n = \sum_p 1/(2^p - 1)$ holds and that every rescaled tail splits as $T(N) = \sum_{j<k} \omega(N+1+j)/2^{j+1} + T(N+k)/2^k$, the tail $T(M)$ is strictly positive for every natural $M$. Here $T(M)$ denotes the rescaled tail $\sum_{j\ge 0} \omega(M+1+j)/2^{j+1}$, a tsum over $\mathbb{R}$ (0 if not summable), and $\omega(n)$ is the number of distinct prime factors of $n$.
