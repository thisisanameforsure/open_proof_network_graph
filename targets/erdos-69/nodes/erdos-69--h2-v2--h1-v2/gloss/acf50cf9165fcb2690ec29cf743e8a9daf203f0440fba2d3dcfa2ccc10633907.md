label: Some rescaled tail times b is not an integer

$$\Bigl(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(n)}{2^{n}} = \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{p}-1}\Bigr) \Rightarrow \forall b\in\mathbb{N},\ b>0\ \ \exists N\in\mathbb{N}\ \ \forall z\in\mathbb{Z}:\ b\cdot\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(N+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} \ne z$$

If the series of $\omega(n)/2^n$ over $n \ge 0$ equals the sum over primes $p$ of $1/(2^p - 1)$, then for every positive natural $b$ there is a natural $N$ such that $b$ times the rescaled tail $\sum_{j\ge 0} \omega(N+1+j)/2^{j+1}$ is not an integer, everything in $\mathbb{R}$ with $\omega(n)$ the number of distinct prime factors of $n$. Every series is Mathlib's `tsum` over $\mathbb{R}$, which is 0 when the family is not summable.
