Erdős's tail argument: split the rescaled tail after $k$ terms, bound the remainder by $\log_2(N+k+1) + 1$, and choose $N, k$ so that $b$ times the tail lands strictly between two integers.

The holes are `hsplit` (the tail is a finite head plus the next tail over $2^k$), `hpos` and `hbound` (the tail is positive and at most $\log_2(M+1) + 1$), and `hcrux` (some $N, k$ with the residue of $b \cdot (\text{head} \cdot 2^k) \bmod 2^k$ plus $b \cdot (\log_2(N+k+1) + 1)$ below $2^k$). The assembly multiplies $b$ times the tail by $2^k$ and shows it lies strictly between $q \cdot 2^k$ and $(q+1) \cdot 2^k$, so it equals no integer $z$.
