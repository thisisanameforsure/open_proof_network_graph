label: Logarithmic bound on the rescaled tail
---
$$\forall M\in\mathbb{N}:\quad \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(M+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} \le \log_{2}(M+1) + 1$$

Under the hypotheses that the Lambert identity $\sum_{n\ge 0} \omega(n)/2^n = \sum_p 1/(2^p - 1)$ holds, that every rescaled tail splits as $T(N) = \sum_{j<k} \omega(N+1+j)/2^{j+1} + T(N+k)/2^k$, and that every $T(M)$ is positive, the tail $T(M)$ is at most $\log_2(M+1) + 1$ for every natural $M$, with $\log_2$ Mathlib's `Real.logb 2`. Here $T(M)$ denotes the rescaled tail $\sum_{j\ge 0} \omega(M+1+j)/2^{j+1}$, a tsum over $\mathbb{R}$ (0 if not summable), and $\omega(n)$ is the number of distinct prime factors of $n$.
