label: Irrationality of the prime sum 1/(2^p - 1)
---
$$\Bigl(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(n)}{2^{n}} = \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{p}-1}\Bigr) \;\Rightarrow\; \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{p}-1}\notin\mathbb{Q}$$

If the series of $\omega(n)/2^n$ over $n \ge 0$ equals the sum over primes $p$ of $1/(2^p - 1)$, then that prime sum is irrational, everything in $\mathbb{R}$ with $\omega(n)$ the number of distinct prime factors of $n$. Every series is Mathlib's `tsum` over $\mathbb{R}$, which is 0 when the family is not summable, and `Irrational x` means $x$ is not the cast of a rational.
