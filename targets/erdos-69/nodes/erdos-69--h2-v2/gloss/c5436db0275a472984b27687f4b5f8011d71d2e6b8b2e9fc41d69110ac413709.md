Erdős 1948: if the series were the rational $q$, every rescaled tail times $q.\mathrm{den}$ would be an integer, but for every $b$ some rescaled tail times $b$ is not.

The one hole is `hB`: for every $b > 0$ there is an $N$ with $b \cdot \sum_j \omega(N+1+j)/2^{j+1}$ not an integer. The assembly proves `hA`, that a rational value $q$ makes $q.\mathrm{den}$ times the rescaled tail the integer $2^N q.\mathrm{num} - \sum_{n\le N} q.\mathrm{den}\,\omega(n)\,2^{N-n}$, and plays the two against each other with $b = q.\mathrm{den}$, passing from the prime sum to the $\omega$ series by the Lambert identity.
