label: A rational series puts dilated tails near integers

$$\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\Bigl\lvert q\,D_a(b+Qt) - \operatorname{round}\bigl(q\,D_a(b+Qt)\bigr)\Bigr\rvert \le q\sum_{p\mid a}\Bigl(\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{T}\Bigr),\qquad D_a(m) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega\bigl(a(m+k+1)\bigr)}{2^{k+1}}$$

For naturals $q$ and an integer $z$ with $q \cdot \sum_{n\ge 0} \omega(n)/2^n = z$, and for naturals $a, Q, b, T$ with $a \ne 0$, $T > 0$ and every prime factor $p$ of $a$ coprime to $Q$, the average over $t < T$ of the distance from $q \cdot D_a(b+Qt)$ to its nearest integer is at most $q$ times the sum over the prime factors $p$ of $a$ of $1/p + 1/T$. Everything is in $\mathbb{R}$: $\omega(n)$ is the number of distinct prime factors of $n$, `round` is Mathlib's rounding (half rounds up), the sum over $p \mid a$ runs over `a.primeFactors`, and each series is a tsum (0 when not summable).
