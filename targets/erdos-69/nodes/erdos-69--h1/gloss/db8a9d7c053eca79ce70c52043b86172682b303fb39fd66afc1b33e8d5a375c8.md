label: Lambert identity for the omega series (untyped casts)

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\uparrow\omega(n)}{2^{n}} = \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{\uparrow p}-1}$$

The series of $\omega(n)/2^n$ over all $n \ge 0$, with $\omega(n)$ the number of distinct prime factors of $n$, equals the sum over primes $p$ of $1/(2^p - 1)$. The cast arrows $\uparrow$ in the Lean carry no target type, so as written the identity is not stated over $\mathbb{R}$, where the parent's hole states it: under Lean's numeral default both sides read in $\mathbb{N}$, where division rounds down and $2^p - 1$ is truncated subtraction, and $\sum'$ is the tsum (0 when not summable).
