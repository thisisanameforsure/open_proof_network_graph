label: Irrationality of the prime indicator in any base
---
$$\forall b\in\mathbb{N},\ b\ge 2:\quad \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{b^{p}} \notin\mathbb{Q}$$

For every natural base $b \ge 2$, the sum over the primes $p$ of $1/b^p$, in $\mathbb{R}$, is irrational. The series is a tsum over $\mathbb{R}$ (0 when not summable), and `Irrational x` means $x$ is not the cast of a rational.
