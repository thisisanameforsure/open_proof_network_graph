label: Closed form for the dilated tail

$$\forall a,m\in\mathbb{N},\ a\ne 0:\quad \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega\bigl(a(m+k+1)\bigr)}{2^{k+1}} = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(m+k+1)}{2^{k+1}} + \omega(a) - \sum_{p\mid a}\frac{2^{\,m \bmod p}}{2^{p}-1}$$

For a nonzero natural $a$ and any natural $m$, the tail of $\omega$ along the multiples $a(m+k+1)$ equals the undilated tail plus $\omega(a)$ minus, for each prime factor $p$ of $a$, $2^{m \bmod p}/(2^p - 1)$, all in $\mathbb{R}$, with $\omega(n)$ the number of distinct prime factors of $n$ and the last sum over `a.primeFactors`. Every series is Mathlib's `tsum` over $\mathbb{R}$, which is 0 when the family is not summable.
