label: Crux window whose residue leaves room below 2^k

$$\forall b\in\mathbb{N},\ b>0\ \ \exists N,k\in\mathbb{N}:\quad \Bigl(b\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}\omega(N+1+j)\,2^{\,k-1-j}\Bigr)\bmod 2^{k} \;+\; b\bigl(\log_{2}(N+k+1)+1\bigr) < 2^{k}$$

Under the hypotheses that the Lambert identity $\sum_{n\ge 0} \omega(n)/2^n = \sum_p 1/(2^p - 1)$ holds, that every rescaled tail splits as $T(N) = \sum_{j<k} \omega(N+1+j)/2^{j+1} + T(N+k)/2^k$, that every $T(M)$ is positive, and that $T(M) \le \log_2(M+1) + 1$ for every $M$, for every positive natural $b$ there are naturals $N$ and $k$ such that the residue $\bmod 2^k$ of the integer $b \cdot \sum_{j<k} \omega(N+1+j) \cdot 2^{k-1-j}$, plus $b(\log_2(N+k+1) + 1)$, is less than $2^k$. The sum, the product and the residue are computed in $\mathbb{N}$ (with $k - 1 - j$ truncated subtraction, which is 0 when $k = 0$) and then cast to $\mathbb{R}$, where $\log_2$ is `Real.logb 2`; here $T(M)$ denotes the rescaled tail $\sum_{j\ge 0} \omega(M+1+j)/2^{j+1}$, a tsum over $\mathbb{R}$ (0 if not summable), and $\omega(n)$ is the number of distinct prime factors of $n$.
