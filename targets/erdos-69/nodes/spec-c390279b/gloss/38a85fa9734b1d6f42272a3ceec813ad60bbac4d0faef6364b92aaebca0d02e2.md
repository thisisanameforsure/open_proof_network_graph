label: Rational binary series has eventually periodic tails mod 1
---
$$\exists p>0,\ N_0\ \ \forall n,n'\ge N_0,\ n\equiv n' \pmod p\ \ \exists z\in\mathbb{Z}:\quad \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{f(n+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} - \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{f(n'+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} = z$$

For every $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and rational $q$, if the family $f(n)/2^n$ is summable in $\mathbb{R}$ and $q$ equals its sum $\sum_{n\ge 0} f(n)/2^n$, then there are a positive natural $p$ and a natural $N_0$ such that any two tails taken from $n, n' \ge N_0$ with $n \equiv n' \pmod{p}$ differ by an integer. The tails are tsums over $\mathbb{R}$ (0 when not summable); the hypotheses are the "if" part and the display is the conclusion.
