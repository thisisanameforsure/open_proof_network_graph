label: Irrationality of the prime sum (untyped casts)

$$\Bigl(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\uparrow\omega(n)}{2^{n}} = \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{\uparrow p}-1}\Bigr) \;\Rightarrow\; \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{p}-1}\notin\mathbb{Q}$$

If the series of $\omega(n)/2^n$ equals the sum over primes of $1/(2^p - 1)$, then that prime sum, taken in $\mathbb{R}$, is irrational. In the hypothesis the cast arrows $\uparrow$ carry no target type, so as written it is not the identity over $\mathbb{R}$ that the parent's hole states: under Lean's numeral default both of its sides read in $\mathbb{N}$, where division rounds down and $2^p - 1$ is truncated subtraction; only the conclusion, being `Irrational`, is over $\mathbb{R}$ ($\sum'$ is the tsum, 0 when not summable).
