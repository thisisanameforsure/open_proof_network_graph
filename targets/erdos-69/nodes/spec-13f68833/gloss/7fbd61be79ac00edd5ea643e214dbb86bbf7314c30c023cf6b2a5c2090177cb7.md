label: Linear bound on omega bounds the tail by 2C
---
$$\forall n\in\mathbb{N},\ C\in\mathbb{R}:\quad \Bigl(\forall k\in\mathbb{N},\ \omega(n+1+k) \le C\,(k+1)\Bigr) \Rightarrow \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(n+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} \le 2C$$

If, for a natural $n$ and a real $C$, $\omega(n+1+k) \le C(k+1)$ for every natural $k$, then the tail $\sum_{j\ge 0} \omega(n+1+j)/2^{j+1}$ is at most $2C$, with $\omega(n)$ the number of distinct prime factors of $n$. Every series is Mathlib's `tsum` over $\mathbb{R}$, which is 0 when the family is not summable.
