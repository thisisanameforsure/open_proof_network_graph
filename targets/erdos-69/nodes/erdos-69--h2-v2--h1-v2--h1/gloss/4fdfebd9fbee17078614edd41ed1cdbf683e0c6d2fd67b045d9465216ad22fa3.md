label: Splitting a rescaled tail after k terms
---
$$\forall N,k\in\mathbb{N}:\quad \sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(N+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} = \sum_{j=0}^{k-1}\frac{\omega(N+1+j)}{2^{j+1}} + \frac{1}{2^{k}}\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(N+k+1+j)}{2^{j+1}}$$

Under the hypothesis that the Lambert identity $\sum_{n\ge 0} \omega(n)/2^n = \sum_p 1/(2^p - 1)$ holds, the rescaled tail $T(N)$ equals its first $k$ terms plus $T(N+k)/2^k$, for all naturals $N$ and $k$. Here $T(M)$ denotes the rescaled tail $\sum_{j\ge 0} \omega(M+1+j)/2^{j+1}$, a tsum over $\mathbb{R}$ (0 if not summable), and $\omega(n)$ is the number of distinct prime factors of $n$.
