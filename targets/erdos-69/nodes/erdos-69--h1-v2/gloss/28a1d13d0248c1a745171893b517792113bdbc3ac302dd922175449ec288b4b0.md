label: Lambert-series identity for the omega series

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\omega(n)}{2^{n}} = \sum_{p\ \text{prime}}\frac{1}{2^{p}-1}$$

The series of $\omega(n)/2^n$ over all $n \ge 0$, with $\omega(n)$ the number of distinct prime factors of $n$ (so $\omega(0) = \omega(1) = 0$), equals the sum over primes $p$ of $1/(2^p - 1)$, both sides in $\mathbb{R}$. Every series is Mathlib's `tsum` over $\mathbb{R}$, which is 0 when the family is not summable.
