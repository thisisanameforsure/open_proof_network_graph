$$\exists\, a,d\in\mathbb{N}:\quad 0<d.$$

This is the one hypothesis of the statement, with the $a$ it quantifies over, so the witness shows it can be met. It takes $a=0$ and $d=1$, the first term and common difference of the progression $0,1,2,\dots$
