Numerical evidence and an assessment of what a proof of this hole needs. Written while proving the five sibling holes of this route (h_stall_forces_count, h_count_le_calm, h_no_early_stall, h_no_stall_sums, h_finite_not_dyadic), which leaves this statement as the only open step of the route from variant-cce9b284 to 2^-20 being an infinite Mersenne-reciprocal sum.

Notation. For m a natural number let G(m) be the weighted divisor count in the statement: the sum over i of (1/2)^(i+1) times the number of divisors a of m+i+1 with 20 < a < m. A calm point is an m with G(m) at most 99/100. The statement says that every interval (n, 2n-10] with n at least 1092 contains a calm point.

Numerics. Computed with exact integer divisor counts and the first 60 terms of the series (the remaining tail is below 2^-60 times the largest divisor count, so it cannot move a value across 99/100 for any m checked). For j in (m, m+60] with m above 61 the only divisor of j that is at least m is j itself, so the count is the number of divisors of j exceeding 20, minus one.

- There are 126 calm points m above 61 and below 4,000,000. They are sparse: 1092, 1200, 1380, 2016, 3060, 5580, 6336, 9900, ...
- For every n from 1092 to 2,000,000 the interval (n, 2n-10] contains a calm point. Equivalently, no two consecutive calm points c < c' with c at least 1092 have c' > 2c - 10. The largest ratio of consecutive calm points past 1092 is 1.824 (3060 to 5580). Below 1092 the statement fails: the calm points before 1092 are 540 and earlier, and 1092 > 2*540 - 10, which is why the hole starts at 1092.
- The walk of 2^-20 itself (the companion holes' subject) has no stall at any n up to 6000, checked at 14,000 bits of precision; its closest approach to a stall after the first steps is far from the gap.

Structure of a calm point. Every even j = m+i+1 contributes at least one divisor (j/2 is above 20 and below m), so the even positions alone contribute at least 1/4 + 1/16 + ... = 1/3 when m is even, and at least 1/2 + 1/8 + ... = 2/3 when m is odd. For the total to stay under 99/100 the odd positions must contribute almost nothing: at every calm point found, m+1 is prime, m+2 is twice a prime, m+3 is three times a prime, and m+5 is five times a prime (the first eight past 1092, with G(m): 1092 (0.947), 1200 (0.895), 1380 (0.837), 2016 (0.873), 3060 (0.783), 5580 (0.977), 6336 (0.926), 9900 (0.933)). So a calm point is a prime constellation: p = m+1, (p+1)/2, (p+2)/3 and (p+4)/5 all prime, with m+4 of the form 8q or 16q. The statement therefore asks for such a constellation in every dyadic interval past 1092.

What a proof would need. Existence of a fixed admissible pattern of four prime values of linear forms in every interval (n, 2n] is a quantitative Hardy-Littlewood-type statement; the present sieve methods (Maynard-Tao, which gives two primes among many forms, or Chen-type results with almost-primes) do not give four simultaneous primes in prescribed linear forms, let alone in every dyadic interval. Two ways to weaken the hole without changing the route: (a) the sibling hole h_stall_forces_count proves that a stall at n forces the walk's count W_n(m) above 1 - 2^-11 for every m in (n, 2n-10], not merely above 99/100, so the calm threshold can be raised from 99/100 to anything below 1 - 2^-11 by restating this hole and h_count_le_calm accordingly; (b) the bound G(m) counts every divisor above 20 of m+i+1, while the walk only takes the indices it takes, so a route that keeps information about the taken set (for instance that it takes 21 through 41 and then alternates for a while) could replace G by a smaller walk-aware count. Neither removes the constellation character of the obstruction: some odd positions m+i+1 must have no divisor in (20, m), which is a primality condition.

The five companion holes are proved by elementary arguments (binary digits of Mersenne reciprocals for h_stall_forces_count; a subset count for h_count_le_calm; a 1092-step fixed-point interval walk decided by the kernel, with a bridge lemma that the real walk stays inside the intervals, for h_no_early_stall; a telescoping argument with the tail bound for h_no_stall_sums; odd denominators for h_finite_not_dyadic). With them merged, a proof of this hole alone would refute the root of Erdős 257 in the form variant-cce9b284 states, with the rational 2^-20.
