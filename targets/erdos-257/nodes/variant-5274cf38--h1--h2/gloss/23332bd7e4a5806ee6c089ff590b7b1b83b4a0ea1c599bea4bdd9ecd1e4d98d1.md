label: m² primes ≡ 1 mod P, product at most 2^(1000 P³ m¹²)

$$\forall P \ge 1\ \forall m \ge 1\ \exists S \subseteq \mathbb{P},\ |S| = m^2,\ \forall p \in S:\ p \equiv 1 \pmod P,\ p > 2m^2 + m,\ \text{and } \prod_{p \in S} p \le 2^{1000 P^3 m^{12}}$$

Provable from the pinned Mathlib: with $n = 6Pm^2$ (not a prime power) and $b_s = 1 + n \cdot B_0! \cdot s$, every prime factor of $\Phi_n(b_s)$ is $\equiv 1 \pmod{n}$ and exceeds $B_0$ ($\Phi_n(b_s) \equiv \Phi_n(1) = 1$ modulo every prime $\le B_0$), and lies below $b_s^n$; the $b_s$ are distinct roots of $X^n - 1$ modulo such a prime, so $B_0 = n \cdot m^2$ values of $s$ give $m^2$ distinct primes (Polynomial.isRoot_cyclotomic_iff, eval_one_cyclotomic_not_prime_pow, card_nthRoots).
