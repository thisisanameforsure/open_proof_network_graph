$$\exists\, P,m\in\mathbb{N}:\quad 0<P,\qquad 1\le m.$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $P=1$ and $m=1$. The set of primes the statement asks for belongs to its conclusion, and the witness says nothing about it.
