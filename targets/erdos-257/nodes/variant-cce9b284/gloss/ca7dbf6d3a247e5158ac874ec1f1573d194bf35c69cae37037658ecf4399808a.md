The witness is the proposition `True`, proved by `trivial`. The statement has no hypotheses (it asserts that an infinite set $A$ and a rational $s$ with $\sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{2^n-1}=s$ exist), so there is nothing whose satisfiability a witness could show.
