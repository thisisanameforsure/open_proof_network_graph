$$A \text{ infinite} \Rightarrow A \in \mathcal{C}_1 \cup \cdots \cup \mathcal{C}_{15} \lor \sum_{n \in A} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \notin \mathbb{Q}$$

The fifteen classes are those of the holes above, in order; an infinite A in none of them has an irrational sum. Stated positively, as a disjunction, so that it can be witnessed.
