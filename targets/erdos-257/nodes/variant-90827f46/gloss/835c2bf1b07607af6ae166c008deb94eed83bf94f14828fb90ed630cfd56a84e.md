$$\exists\, r\in\mathbb{N},\ c:\{0,\dots,r-1\}\times\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}:\quad 0<r,\qquad \forall i:\ 0<c_{i,0},\qquad \forall i,j:\ \ (2r+1)^{r+1}\,c_{i,j}\le c_{i,j+1}.$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $r=1$ and the single sequence $c_{0,j}=9^j$: it starts at $1$, and with $r=1$ the required ratio is $(2\cdot 1+1)^{2}=9$, so the inequality reads $9\cdot 9^j\le 9^{j+1}$.
