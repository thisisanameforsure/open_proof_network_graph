label: union of finitely many lacunary sequences
---
$$\forall\, r \ge 1,\ \forall\, c_0, \dots, c_{r-1} : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N},\quad \Big(c_i(0) > 0,\ \ (2r+1)^{r+1}\, c_i(j) \le c_i(j+1)\ \text{for all } i, j\Big) \;\Longrightarrow\; \sum_{n \in \bigcup_i \{c_i(j) : j \in \mathbb{N}\}} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \notin \mathbb{Q}.$$

The sum is over the union of the $r$ sequences' values, each value counted once. Each sequence grows by a factor of at least $(2r+1)^{r+1}$ per step (9 for one sequence, 125 for two, 2401 for three); nothing is assumed about divisibility or about how the sequences sit relative to each other. Every element is at least $1$, so no term meets Lean's junk value at $n = 0$.
