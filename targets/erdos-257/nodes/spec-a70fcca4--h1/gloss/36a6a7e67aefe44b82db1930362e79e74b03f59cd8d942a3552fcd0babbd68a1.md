$$\exists\, r:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R},\ \exists\, m:\mathbb{N}:\quad r_0=\tfrac12\ \wedge\ \forall m:\ r_{m+1}=\begin{cases} r_m-\dfrac{1}{2^m-1} & \text{if } \dfrac{1}{2^m-1}\le r_m,\\[4pt] r_m & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$

The witness is the greedy remainder of $\tfrac12$ itself, defined by recursion on $m$ (`Nat.rec`), so both equations hold by definition (`rfl`), and $m = 0$, of which nothing is required. Lean convention: $\frac{1}{2^0-1} = \frac10 = 0$, so step $0$ is taken and subtracts nothing.
