label: greedy remainder bookkeeping

$$\forall\, r:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R}:\quad r_0=\tfrac12,\ \ \forall m:\ r_{m+1}=\begin{cases} r_m-\dfrac{1}{2^m-1} & \text{if } \dfrac{1}{2^m-1}\le r_m,\\[4pt] r_m & \text{otherwise}\end{cases} \ \Longrightarrow\ \forall m:\ \ 0\le r_m \ \text{ and } \sum_{\substack{a<m\\ \frac{1}{2^a-1}\le r_a}}\frac{1}{2^a-1}=\frac12-r_m .$$

$r_m$ is the remainder of the greedy walk for $\frac12$ before position $m$, and the sum runs over the positions $a < m$ the walk has taken. The statement is bookkeeping: the remainder stays nonnegative and is one half minus what has been taken. Lean conventions: at $a = 0$ the term is $\frac10 = 0$, so position $0$ is "taken" at no cost and changes neither side.
