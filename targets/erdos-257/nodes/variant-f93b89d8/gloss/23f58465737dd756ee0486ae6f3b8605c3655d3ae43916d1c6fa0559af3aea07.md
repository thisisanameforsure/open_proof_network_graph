label: powers of two bases
---
$$\forall\, a, b \in \mathbb{N},\quad 2 \le a < b \;\Longrightarrow\; \sum_{n \in \{a^k : k \in \mathbb{N}\} \cup \{b^k : k \in \mathbb{N}\}} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \notin \mathbb{Q}.$$

The sum is over the set of all powers of $a$ and of $b$, including $1 = a^0 = b^0$, counted once, as the sum is over a set; every element is at least $1$, so no term meets Lean's junk value at $n = 0$. The bases need not be coprime or multiplicatively independent.
