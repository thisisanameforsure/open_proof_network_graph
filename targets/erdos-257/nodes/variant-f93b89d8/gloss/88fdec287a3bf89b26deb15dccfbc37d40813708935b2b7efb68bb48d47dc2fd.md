$$\Bigl(\forall\, A \subseteq \mathbb{N}\ \text{infinite}:\ \sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{2^n-1}\notin\mathbb{Q}\Bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; \Bigl(\forall\, a,b\in\mathbb{N},\ 2\le a,\ a<b:\ \ \sum_{n\in\{a^k\,:\,k\in\mathbb{N}\}\cup\{b^k\,:\,k\in\mathbb{N}\}}\frac{1}{2^n-1}\notin\mathbb{Q}\Bigr).$$

The hypothesis is the root statement; the conclusion is this node's statement. So the file proves that the root implies this node.

The Lean fixes $a$ and $b$ and applies the root to the union of the two sets of powers, so it has to check that the union is infinite. It contains the powers of $a$, which are the range of the map $k\mapsto a^k$, and that map is injective because $a\ge 2$. The hypothesis $a<b$ is not used. The node is the root at a family of particular sets.
