$$\sum_{n \ \text{squarefree}} \frac{1}{2^n-1} \ \text{ is irrational}.$$

The sum runs over Mathlib's `Squarefree` natural numbers: $1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, \dots$ ($0$ is not squarefree in Mathlib, $1$ is). The sum is Lean's `tsum`, the ordinary sum of a convergent positive series.
