$$r_m \le \sum_{k\ge 0} \frac{1}{2^{k+m}-1}$ for every $m$, for the greedy remainder $r$ of $\frac12$$

The open mathematics. The remainder before position m never exceeds the whole tail of the series from m on, so the walk can always continue; equivalently (given the holes above) the remainder tends to 0. Verified numerically to depth 10^8 (Hong 2026), no proof known. A stall at m would be r m > tail m, after which 1/2 is not a subset sum at all.
