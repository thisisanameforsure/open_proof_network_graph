label: eventually periodic A (Luca–Tachiya 2014)

$$\forall\, A \subseteq \mathbb{N} \text{ infinite}:\quad \bigl(\exists\, p \ge 1,\ N:\ \forall n \ge N,\ n \in A \iff n + p \in A\bigr) \ \Longrightarrow\ \sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{2^n-1} \ \text{ is irrational}.$$

The hypothesis says the indicator of $A$ is eventually periodic with period $p$. Sums are Lean's `tsum` over the set, the ordinary sum of a convergent nonnegative series; the term at $n = 0$ is Lean's $\frac{1}{2^0-1} = \frac10 = 0$.
