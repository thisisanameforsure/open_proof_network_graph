$$\Bigl(\forall\, A \subseteq \mathbb{N} \text{ infinite},\ \sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{2^n-1} \notin \mathbb{Q}\Bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2^n-1} \notin \mathbb{Q}.$$

The root implies this variant (relation `partial`): take $A = \mathbb{N}$, which is infinite, and identify the sum over the set $\mathbb{N}$ with the sum over $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Lean's term at $n = 0$ is $\frac10 = 0$ on both sides.
