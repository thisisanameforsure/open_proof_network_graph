$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2^n-1} \ \text{ is irrational}.$$

The case $A = \mathbb{N}$ of the root. In Lean the term at $n = 0$ is $\frac{1}{2^0-1} = \frac10 = 0$, so the sum is $\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{1}{2^n-1}$; the series converges, and Lean's `tsum` is its ordinary sum.
