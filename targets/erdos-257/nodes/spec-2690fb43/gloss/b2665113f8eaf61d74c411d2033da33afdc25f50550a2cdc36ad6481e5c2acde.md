$$\neg\Big(\forall n \ge 1092\ \exists m:\ n < m,\ m+10 \le 2n,\ G(m) \le \tfrac{99}{100}\Big)\ \vee\ \Big(\forall n \ge 1092\ \exists q \text{ prime}:\ \big(n < 2q-2,\ 2q+8 \le 2n,\ 2q-1 \text{ prime}\big) \vee \big(n < 4q-2,\ 4q+8 \le 2n,\ 4q-1 \text{ prime}\big)\Big),$$
where $G(m) = \sum_{i \ge 0} 2^{-(i+1)}\,\#\{a : 20 < a < m,\ a \mid m+i+1\}$.

Lean conventions: the implication "the calm-point hole implies the prime-pair statement" is written as $\neg H \vee P$, classically the same thing. The subtractions $2q-1$, $2q-2$, $4q-1$, $4q-2$ are on natural numbers but exact, since $q$ prime gives $q \ge 2$. The counts are `Set.ncard` of subsets of $(20, m)$, finite, so they are true sizes.

The left disjunct negates the statement of the hole `variant-cce9b284--h6` verbatim; the right disjunct is the open prime-pair statement `spec-001a7622`. So this node is the kernel-checked barrier: a proof of the calm-point hole would prove, in every interval $(n, 2n-10]$, a prime $q$ with $2q-1$ or $4q-1$ also prime, a quantitative two-form case of Dickson's conjecture that is not known even in its infinitude form. The proof shows a calm $m \ge 1093$ has $m+1$ prime and $m+2 = 2q$ or $4q$ with $q$ prime, by bounding the first ten terms of $G(m)$ below through the small divisors $2,\dots,24$ of each $m+i+1$, a residue check modulo 720 decided in the kernel.
