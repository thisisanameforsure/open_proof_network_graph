$$\exists j\ge 1,\ N\ge j:\ \text{no stall at } n+1 \text{ for } n<N,\ \text{ and } \forall n\ge N\ \exists m:\ n<m,\ m+10\le 2n,\ G_j(m)\le\tfrac{99}{100}$$

The walk is the greedy walk of 2^-j. The second part is about the divisors of m+1, m+2, ... only.
