label: the Mersenne reciprocal series converges

$$\sum_{n\ge 0} \frac{1}{2^n-1} < \infty$$

(summable; the $n = 0$ term is Lean's $0$.) Comparison with the geometric series: $1/(2^n-1) \le 2/2^n$ for $n \ge 1$.
