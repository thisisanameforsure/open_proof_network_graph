## Overview

The assembly proves uniqueness of representation, $A = B$ whenever $0 \notin A$, $0 \notin B$ and $\sum_{n \in A} t_n = \sum_{n \in B} t_n$ with $t_n = \frac{1}{2^n - 1}$, from two holes and the gap lemma of spec-ddd99827, $\sum_{k \ge 0} t_{n+1+k} < t_n$ for $n \ge 1$ (`Opn.erdos_257_tail_lt_term`). Arguing by contradiction, it compares the two sums at the least element of the symmetric difference of $A$ and $B$.

## The holes {steps: h1 h3}

Hole `h1` (node spec-5e2e0890--h1): $(t_m)_{m \ge 0}$ is summable. Hole `h3` (node spec-5e2e0890--h2): for $g \ge 0$ summable, sets $S, T$ and $n \in S \setminus T$ with $S$ and $T$ agreeing below $n$, if $\sum_{k \ge 0} g(n+1+k) < g(n)$ then $\sum_{m \in T} g(m) < \sum_{m \in S} g(m)$.

## The least point of disagreement {steps: hex hn hmin}

If $A \ne B$, some $n$ lies in exactly one of them (`hex`, by extensionality). Let $n_0$ be the least such $n$ (`Nat.find`); it lies in exactly one of $A$, $B$ (`hn`), and every $m < n_0$ is in $A$ exactly when it is in $B$ (`hmin`, by minimality).

## Nonnegativity {steps: hnonneg}

$2^m \ge 1$, so $t_m \ge 0$ for every $m$.

## The two cases {steps: s7 s8}

If $n_0 \in A \setminus B$: since $0 \notin A$, $n_0 \ge 1$, so the gap lemma gives $\sum_{k} t_{n_0+1+k} < t_{n_0}$, and `h3` with $g = t$, $S = A$, $T = B$ gives $\sum_{B} t < \sum_{A} t$, contradicting the equality of the sums. If $n_0 \in B \setminus A$, the same argument with the roles exchanged gives $\sum_{A} t < \sum_{B} t$.
