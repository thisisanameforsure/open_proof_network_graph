$$\sum_{m \ge 0} \frac{1}{2^m - 1} < \infty \text{ (with Lean's convention } 1/0 = 0 \text{ at } m = 0)$$

Comparison with a geometric series: for $m \ge 1$, $1/(2^m - 1) \le 2 \cdot (1/2)^m$.
