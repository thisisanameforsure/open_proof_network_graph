$$\exists\, A,B\subseteq\mathbb{N}:\quad 0\notin A,\quad 0\notin B,\quad \sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{2^n-1}=\sum_{n\in B}\frac{1}{2^n-1}.$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $A=B=\emptyset$: $0$ is in neither, and the two sums are the same expression.
