$$\exists\, q\in\mathbb{N},\ T\in\mathbb{R},\ \delta\in\mathbb{R},\ P_0\in\mathbb{N}:\quad 0<q,\qquad 0<\delta.$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, with the $T$ and $P_0$ it quantifies over, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $q=1$, $T=0$, $\delta=1$ and $P_0=0$. The hypotheses on $p_0$, $v$ and $P$ that appear later, inside the statement's conclusion, are not part of the witness.
