$$\exists\, r\in\mathbb{Q},\ G\subseteq\mathbb{N}:\quad 0\notin G\ \ \text{and}\ \ \forall n\ge 1:\ \Bigl(n\in G \iff \frac{1}{2^n-1}\le r-\sum_{a\in G,\ a<n}\frac{1}{2^a-1}\Bigr).$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $r=0$ and $G=\emptyset$: the sum is empty, and for $n\ge 1$ the term $\frac{1}{2^n-1}$ is positive, so it is not at most $0$ and $n\notin G$, as required.
