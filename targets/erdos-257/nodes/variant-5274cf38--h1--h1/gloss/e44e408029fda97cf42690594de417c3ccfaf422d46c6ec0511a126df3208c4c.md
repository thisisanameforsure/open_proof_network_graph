label: m² primes ≡ 1 mod P between 2^m and 2^(m+m²)

$$\forall P \ge 1\ \exists m_0\ \forall m \ge m_0\ \exists S \subseteq \mathbb{P},\ |S| = m^2,\ \forall p \in S:\ p \equiv 1 \pmod P,\ 2^m < p \le 2^{m + m^2}$$

A weak quantitative Dirichlet theorem: the prime number theorem in arithmetic progressions gives $\asymp 2^{m+m^2}/(\varphi(P)\, m^2)$ such primes. Mathlib has Dirichlet's theorem only qualitatively; the cyclotomic argument gives a prime $\equiv 1 \pmod{n}$ below $(n+1)^n$, which is too sparse for the averaging step.
