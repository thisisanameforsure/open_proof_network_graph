$$\exists\, P\in\mathbb{N}:\quad 0<P.$$

This is the one hypothesis of the statement, so the witness shows it can be met. It takes $P=1$. The primes congruent to $1$ modulo $P$ belong to the statement's conclusion, and the witness says nothing about them.
