label: powers of two and three ⇒ irrational
---
$$\sum_{n \in \{2^k : k \in \mathbb{N}\} \cup \{3^k : k \in \mathbb{N}\}} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \notin \mathbb{Q}.$$

The sum is over the set of all powers of $2$ and of $3$ (including $1 = 2^0 = 3^0$, counted once, as the sum is over a set); every element is at least $1$, so no term meets Lean's junk value at $n = 0$.
