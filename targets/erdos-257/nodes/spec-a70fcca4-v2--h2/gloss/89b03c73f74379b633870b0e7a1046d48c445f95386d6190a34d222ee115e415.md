$$0\le Z_j\le C(j+1)\ \forall j\ \Rightarrow\ \sum_{m}\frac{d_A(m)}{2^m}=\frac{p}{q}$$

Here d_A(m) counts the elements of A dividing m, and the carry is the integer Z_j = p 2^j - q (d_A(1) 2^(j-1) + ... + d_A(j)), which satisfies Z_(j+1) = 2 Z_j - q d_A(j+1). If every carry is nonnegative and they grow at most linearly, the expansion sums to p/q.
