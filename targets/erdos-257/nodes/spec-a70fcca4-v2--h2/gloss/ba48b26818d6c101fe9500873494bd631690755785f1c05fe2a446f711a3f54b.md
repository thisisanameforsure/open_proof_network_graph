label: carry criterion

$$0\le Z_j\le C(j+1)\ \forall j\ \Rightarrow\ \sum_{m}\frac{d_A(m)}{2^m}=\frac{p}{q}$$

Here $d_A(m)$ counts the elements of $A$ dividing $m$, and the carry is the integer $Z_j = p\,2^j - q\,\bigl(d_A(1)\,2^{j-1} + \dots + d_A(j)\bigr)$, which satisfies $Z_{j+1} = 2Z_j - q\,d_A(j+1)$. If every carry is nonnegative and they grow at most linearly, the expansion sums to $p/q$.
