$$\exists x\in\mathbb{Q}_{>0}:\ A(x)\ \text{infinite}\ \wedge\ \sum_{m}\bigl(q_m-\mathbf 1_{A(x)}(m)\bigr)\frac{1}{2^m-1}\in\mathbb{Q}$$

Some positive rational x whose walk (r_0 = x, q_m = ⌊r_m (2^m - 1)⌋, r_{m+1} = r_m - q_m/(2^m - 1)) chooses infinitely many indices and whose repair sum, the subtracted terms counted with multiplicity, is a rational number. Two ways to meet it: only finitely many repairs, or infinitely many whose sum is rational all the same.
