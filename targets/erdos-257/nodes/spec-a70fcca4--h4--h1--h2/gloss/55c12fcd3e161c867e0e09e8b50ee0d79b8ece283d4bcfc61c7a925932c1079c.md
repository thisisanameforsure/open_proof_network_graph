$$\exists\, r:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R},\ \exists\, m:\mathbb{N}:\quad r_0=\tfrac12\ \wedge\ \forall m:\ r_{m+1}=\begin{cases} r_m-\dfrac{1}{2^m-1} & \text{if } \dfrac{1}{2^m-1}\le r_m,\\[4pt] r_m & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}\ \wedge\ 1\le m\ \wedge\ r_m<\frac{1}{2^m-1}$$

The witness is the greedy remainder of $\tfrac12$ itself, defined by recursion on $m$ (`Nat.rec`): $r_0 = \tfrac12$ and the step rule as written, so both equations hold by definition (`rfl`). Lean convention: at $m = 0$ the term is $\frac{1}{2^0-1} = \frac10 = 0$, so step $0$ is taken and subtracts nothing; $r_1 = \tfrac12$. The skipped step exhibited is $m = 1$: $r_1 = \tfrac12 < \frac{1}{2^1-1} = 1$ (`norm_num`).
