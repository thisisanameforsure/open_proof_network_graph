label: after a skipped m >= 1, another skip within (m, 3m+2]

$$m \ge 1 \ \wedge\ r_m < \frac{1}{2^m-1} \ \Longrightarrow\ \exists n,\ m < n \le 3m+2 \ \wedge\ r_n < \frac{1}{2^n-1}$$

Here $r$ is the greedy remainder of $1/2$: $r_0 = 1/2$, $r_{m+1} = r_m - 1/(2^m-1)$ if that term fits under $r_m$, else $r_m$. In words: the set the walk chooses never contains the whole block $m+1, \dots, 3m+2$ right after an element $m \ge 1$ it skipped. This is weaker than the hole of graph PR #865 (window $(m, 2m]$, $m \ge 2$) and essentially equivalent to this node: conversely, the node's bound at a skipped $m$ forces a skip within $(m, 3m+3]$, since the terms over $(m, 3m+3]$ sum to more than $2^{-m} + 4^{-m}/3$ (both inequalities checked exactly for $m \le 120$, margins $3/28$ and $1/56$ times $8^{-m}$).
