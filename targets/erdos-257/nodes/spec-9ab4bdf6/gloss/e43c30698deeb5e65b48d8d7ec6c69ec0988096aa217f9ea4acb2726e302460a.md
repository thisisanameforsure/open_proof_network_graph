$$\forall m \in \mathbb{N},\quad m \ge 1092 \ \wedge\ m+1 \text{ prime} \ \Longrightarrow\ \#\{a : 20 < a < m,\ a \mid m+3\} \le 4 \ \vee\ \frac{99}{100} < \sum_{i \ge 0} 2^{-(i+1)}\, \#\{a : 20 < a < m,\ a \mid m+i+1\}.$$

Lean conventions: the counts are `Set.ncard` of subsets of $(20, m)$, so every set is finite and `ncard` is its true size; the series is a `tsum` of nonnegative terms bounded by $m\,2^{-(i+1)}$, so it converges and `tsum` is its true value.

The series on the right is $G(m)$, the walk-free weighted divisor count of the calm-point hole `variant-cce9b284--h6`; a calm point is an $m$ with $G(m) \le 99/100$. Together with `spec-23231427` (a calm $m \ge 1092$ has $m+1$ prime) the statement says a calm point has at most four divisors of $m+3$ in $(20, m)$, the first step of condition N1 in that hole's annex.

Why it holds: $m+1$ is an odd prime, so $m$ is even and each even $m+i+1$ contributes its half. Five divisors of $m+3$ give $5/8$ by themselves. If $4 \mid m+2$, then $(m+2)/2$ and $(m+2)/4$ give $2/4$, and $5/8 + 1/2 > 99/100$; otherwise $4 \mid m+4$, and $(m+2)/2$, $(m+4)/2$, $(m+4)/4$ give $1/4 + 2/16$, a total of $1 > 99/100$.
