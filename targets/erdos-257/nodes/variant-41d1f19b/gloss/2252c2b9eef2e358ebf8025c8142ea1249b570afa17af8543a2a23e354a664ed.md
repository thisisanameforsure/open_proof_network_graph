$$\exists\, A\subseteq\mathbb{N}\ \text{infinite},\ 0\notin A:\quad \forall C\ \exists m\in A\ \exists F\subseteq\mathbb{N}\ \text{finite}:\ \ F=\{a\in A: a<m\}\quad\text{and}\quad C+\sum_{a\in F}a\le m.$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $A=\{4^k : k\in\mathbb{N}\}$, and for each $C$ it takes $m=4^C$ and $F=\{4^j : j<C\}$. It first proves by induction that $k+\sum_{j<k}4^j\le 4^k$ for every $k$, which at $k=C$ is the required inequality.
