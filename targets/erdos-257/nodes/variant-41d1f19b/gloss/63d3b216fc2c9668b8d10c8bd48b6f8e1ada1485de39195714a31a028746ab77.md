label: long gaps ⇒ irrational

$$\forall A \subseteq \mathbb{N}:\quad A \text{ infinite},\ 0 \notin A,\ \Bigl(\forall C \in \mathbb{N}\ \exists m \in A:\ C + \sum_{a \in A,\ a < m} a \le m\Bigr) \ \Longrightarrow\ \sum_{n \in A} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \notin \mathbb{Q}.$$

In the Lean the finite set $\{a \in A : a < m\}$ is given as a `Finset` $F$ with $a \in F \iff a \in A \wedge a < m$. The sum on the right is over the elements of $A$; $0 \notin A$ excludes Lean's junk value at $n = 0$.
