$$\exists x\in\mathbb{Q}_{>0},\ 2\mid\operatorname{den}(x):\ \#\{m: q_m-\mathbf 1_{A(x)}(m)\ne 0\}<\infty$$

Some positive rational with even denominator whose walk (r_0 = x, q_m = ⌊r_m (2^m - 1)⌋, r_{m+1} = r_m - q_m/(2^m - 1)) makes only finitely many repairs: at all but finitely many indices the term is subtracted at most once, and then as a chosen term.
