## What the assembly shows

The node is the form of Tao and Teräväinen's Theorem 5.1 (arXiv:2512.01739v2) that the primes case needs. The assembly proves it with $c_0=q^2/8$ from one hole (node `variant-6b364d2b--h1--h1`), which collects the choice of the integers $n$, the split of the primes and four estimates. As of 12 November 2026 it is open, so this node is not proved.

Proved in the file before the theorem:

- A quantitative Lemma 5.2: for large $P$, whenever $2^K,H\le(\log P)^{2/3}$ there are $p_0,v_1,\dots,v_K$ such that the $2^K$ numbers $p_\varepsilon=p_0+\sum_k\varepsilon_kv_k$ are distinct primes in $(P/2,P]$ and the shifts $r_{\varepsilon,h+K}=p_\varepsilon(h+K)-\sum_kk\varepsilon_kv_k$, $1\le h\le H$, are pairwise distinct. The proof finds the cube among the at least $n/(10\log n)$ primes of $(n,2n]$ by induction on $K$, each $v_k$ a popular difference.
- Mertens' second theorem on an interval, $\bigl|\sum_{m<p\le n}\frac1p-(\log\log n-\log\log m)\bigr|\le C/\log m$ for $2\le m\le n$, from Mertens' first theorem by partial summation.
- Four lemmas about $X_p(n)=q\sum_{k<H}2^{-(k+K+1)}\sum_\varepsilon(-1)^{|\varepsilon|}1_{p\mid n+r_{\varepsilon,k+K+1}}$, used in the last section.

## The hole: the set-up and four estimates {steps: tt_sec5_setup}

For every $q\ge1$, real $T$, $\delta>0$ and $P_0$ there are $P\ge P_0$ and $K,H\ge1$ with $2^K,H\le(\log P)^{2/3}$ such that, for every cube of primes in $(P/2,P]$ with distinct vertices and distinct shifts, there are a finite nonempty set $N$ of integers $n$ with $n-\sum_kk\varepsilon_kv_k=p_\varepsilon m_\varepsilon(n)$, $m_\varepsilon(n)\in\mathbb{N}$, and $1\le n+r_{\varepsilon,k+K+1}\le B$ for $k<H$; disjoint sets $S_1,S_2$ of primes up to $B$; an even $M$; $0\le\eta\le\frac14$; and $3\le w\le R$, with:

- all $n\in N$ congruent modulo each prime up to $B$ outside $S_1\cup S_2$, and no prime of $S_1$ dividing a difference of two distinct shifts;
- for every $a$, at most $(1+\eta)|N|/p_\varepsilon$ of the $n$ have $p_\varepsilon\mid m_\varepsilon(n)+a$, and for $p\in S_1$ the number with $p\mid n+a$ is within $\eta|N|/p$ of $|N|/p$;
- $q/2^{K+1}\le\delta$ and $\sum_\varepsilon(1+\eta)/p_\varepsilon\le\delta$;
- the four quantities the Lean comments call $\kappa_2,\dots,\kappa_5$ are at most $\delta$;
- $2^KT\le\log\log R-\log\log w$, and the primes of $(w,R]$ missing from $S_1$ have $\sum\frac1p\le1$.

## Constants {steps: s2 hC0}

Let $C$ be the constant of Mertens' theorem on intervals. At $m=n=2$ it gives $0\le C/\log2$, so $C\ge0$.

## The cube and the set-up {steps: s4 s5 s6 hP hpr s9 h_x3b71 hpos hS1p}

Given $q$ and $\delta$, take $P_0$ from Lemma 5.2 and apply the hole with $T=C+2$. Lemma 5.2 at the resulting $P,K,H$ gives a cube of primes $p_\varepsilon$, to which the hole's second half applies. Here $\eta\le1$, the shifted values are positive, and $S_1$ consists of primes.

## Enough primes in $S_1$ {steps: hlogw hmert hCw hIoc hlow hs hc0'}

Since $w\ge3$, $\log w\ge1$, so Mertens gives $\sum_{w<p\le R}\frac1p\ge 2^K(C+2)-C$, and removing the primes missing from $S_1$, $\sum_{p\in S_1}\frac1p\ge2^K(C+2)-C-1\ge2^K$. With $\eta\le\frac14$ and $\sum_{p\in S_1}p^{-2}\le1$ this yields

$$\frac{q^2}{8}\le(1-\eta)\frac{q^2}{2^{K+2}}\sum_{p\in S_1}\frac1p-\eta^2q^2\sum_{p\in S_1}\frac1{p^2}.$$

## Assembling Theorem 5.1 {steps: close}

The objects of the hole are the witnesses. $X_p$ is constant on $N$ for primes outside $S_1\cup S_2$ because, for positive shifted values, $X_p(n)$ depends only on $n$ modulo $p$. For $p\in S_1$ no two cells $(k,\varepsilon)$ have $p\mid n+r_{\varepsilon,k+K+1}$, so $|X_p(n)|\le q/2^{K+1}\le\delta$. The count modulo $p_\varepsilon$ gives $\kappa_1\le\sum_\varepsilon(1+\eta)/p_\varepsilon\le\delta$. The bounds on $\kappa_2,\dots,\kappa_5$ are the hole's. For $p\in S_1$ the count modulo $p$ gives $\operatorname{Var}X_p\ge(1-\eta)\frac{q^2}{2^{K+2}p}-\frac{\eta^2q^2}{p^2}$ (using $K,H\ge1$); summed over $S_1$ this is the right side of the display, hence at least $q^2/8$.
