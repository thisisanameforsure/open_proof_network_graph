$$\exists\, q\in\mathbb{N}:\quad 0<q.$$

This is the one hypothesis of the statement, so the witness shows it can be met. It takes $q=1$. Everything else in the statement, from the constant $c_0$ to the sets, sequences and inequalities that follow it, belongs to the conclusion, and the witness says nothing about it.
