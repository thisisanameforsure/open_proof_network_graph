$$0 \notin A,\ \sum_{a\in A} \frac{1}{2^a-1} = r \in \mathbb{Q} \implies \forall n \ge 1:\ n \in A \iff \frac{1}{2^n-1} \le r - \sum_{a \in A,\, a<n} \frac{1}{2^a-1}$$

Bookkeeping: if n ∈ A the remainder after the terms below n is the term at n plus a nonnegative tail; if n ∉ A it is the tail over a > n, which the gap lemma (spec-ddd99827) bounds strictly below the term at n. Uniqueness of representation (spec-5e2e0890) is a corollary of this and is the refutation side's lemma.
