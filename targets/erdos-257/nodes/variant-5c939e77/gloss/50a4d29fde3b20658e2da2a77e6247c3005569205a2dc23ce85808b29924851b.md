The witness is the proposition `True`, proved by `trivial`. The statement has no hypotheses (it asserts that the family of sets $A\subseteq\mathbb{N}$ with $0\notin A$ and $\sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{2^n-1}$ rational is countable), so there is nothing whose satisfiability a witness could show.
