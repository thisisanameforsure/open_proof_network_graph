$$\sum_{m\in A}\frac{1}{2^m-1}=x-\sum_{m}\bigl(q_m-\mathbf 1_A(m)\bigr)\frac{1}{2^m-1}$$

The walk from a rational x ≥ 0: r_0 = x, q_m = ⌊r_m (2^m - 1)⌋ counts how many times the term 1/(2^m - 1) is subtracted at index m, and r_{m+1} = r_m - q_m/(2^m - 1) is the remainder carried to the next index. A is the set of indices whose last subtraction leaves the remainder within the tail Σ_{j>m} 1/(2^j - 1), the chosen terms; every other subtraction is a repair. The chosen sum is x less the repair sum.
