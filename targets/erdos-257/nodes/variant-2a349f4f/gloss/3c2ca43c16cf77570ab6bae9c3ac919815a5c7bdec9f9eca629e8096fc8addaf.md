label: the subset sums form a Cantor set
---
$$C = \Bigl\{\, \sum_{n \in A} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \;:\; A \subseteq \mathbb{N} \,\Bigr\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}\ \text{ is compact and perfect, and } \operatorname{int} C = \emptyset .$$

Here $A$ ranges over all subsets of $\mathbb{N} = \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$, finite or infinite, and the topology is the usual one on $\mathbb{R}$. Lean conventions: `Perfect` means closed with no isolated points (every point of $C$ is a limit of other points of $C$); `interior` is the topological interior in $\mathbb{R}$. The sum is `tsum` over the elements of $A$, a genuine convergent sum for every $A$. At $n = 0$ the divisor $2^0 - 1$ is $0$ and Lean's $1/0$ is $0$, so $A$ and $A \setminus \{0\}$ give the same sum.
