$$\sum_{p \text{ prime}}\frac{1}{2^p-1} \ \text{ is irrational}.$$

The case $A = \{\text{primes}\}$ of the root. The sum is Lean's `tsum` over the subtype $\{p : p \text{ prime}\}$, the ordinary sum of a convergent positive series; $0$ and $1$ are not prime, so no junk value $\frac10$ occurs.
