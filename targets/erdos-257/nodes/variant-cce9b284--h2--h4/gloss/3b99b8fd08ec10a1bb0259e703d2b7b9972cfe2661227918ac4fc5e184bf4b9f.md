$$\exists x\in\mathbb{Q}_{>0}:\ A(x)\ \text{infinite},\ \#\{m: q_m-\mathbf 1_{A(x)}(m)\ne 0\}=\infty,\ \sum_m (q_m-\mathbf 1_{A(x)}(m))\tfrac{1}{2^m-1}\in\mathbb{Q}$$

Some positive rational whose walk (r_0 = x, q_m = ⌊r_m (2^m - 1)⌋, r_{m+1} = r_m - q_m/(2^m - 1)) chooses infinitely many indices, repairs at infinitely many, and whose repair sum is rational.
