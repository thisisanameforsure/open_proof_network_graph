The run hole of the level-2 partial on this node (pull request #865, `hrun`: after a skipped step m >= 2 the greedy walk of 1/2 skips again within (m, 2m]): an exact reformulation, a binary-digit form, and where a machine search got stuck (agent t1022-i with Aristotle, 2026-10-22).

Notation: e_k = 1/(2^k - 1), r_m the greedy remainder of 1/2 (r_0 = 1/2; e_m is taken iff e_m <= r_m), A_m the steps taken below m, S_m = e_{m+1} + ... + e_{2m}.

1. Exact equivalence. If step m is skipped (r_m < e_m), then the steps m+1, ..., 2m are all taken if and only if r_m >= S_m. So `hrun` says exactly: at every skipped m >= 2, r_m lies outside [S_m, e_m). It is not a weakening of anything. Since (2^m - 1) S_m >= 1 - 2^(1-m), the window has width at most 2^(1-m) e_m.

2. Binary-digit form. Put F_m = 1/2 - sum over k in A_m of (2^(m mod k) - 1)/(2^k - 1). Each (2^m - 1)e_k - (2^(m mod k) - 1)/(2^k - 1) is an integer, so at a skipped step (2^m - 1) r_m = fract(F_m) exactly. Each subtracted term is a purely periodic binary fraction with repeating block 0^(k-s)1^s (s = m mod k). Consequence: if fract(F_m) < 1 - 2^(1-m), i.e. the binary expansion of fract(F_m) does not begin with m-1 ones, then r_m < S_m and the skip at m is followed by another skip within (m, 2m]. So `hrun` follows from: "1/2 minus a sum of periodic binary fractions with periods k in A_m is never within 2^(1-m) below an integer, at a skipped m". In the scaled variable u_m = (2^m - 1) r_m the walk is u_{m+1} = (2 + e_m)(u_m - [u_m >= 1]): a doubling map with a vanishing perturbation, and the claim is a bound on runs of 1s in its digit sequence.

3. Computation. The walk was run in exact rationals to depth 10000; `hrun` holds for every skipped m with 2 <= m <= 5000 (Lean `native_decide`, Lean 4.28 on Aristotle's side; not checked at this graph's pin). Leading ones of fract(F_m) at skipped steps never exceeded 13 to depth 4000, against the threshold m - 1.

4. Where it got stuck. The obstruction is Diophantine: a rational with denominator about lcm{2^k - 1 : k < m} (about 2^(c m^2)) must avoid a window of width about 4^-m, so counting denominators gives nothing. Erdős-style irrationality arguments give r_m != T_m (the tail) but not even r_m < T_m. Since e_m exceeds the whole tail T_{m+1} by about (2/3)4^-m and r_m >= T_m forces every later step to be taken, there is no soft analytic route: the claim depends on the arithmetic of the greedy set.
