$$\#\{m: q_m-\mathbf 1_A(m)\ne 0\}<\infty\ \Rightarrow\ 2\nmid\operatorname{den}(x)\ \text{ or }\ \bigl(A\ \text{infinite}\wedge \sum_m (q_m-\mathbf 1_A(m))\tfrac{1}{2^m-1}\in\mathbb{Q}\bigr)$$

Given the walk of x ≥ 0, the accounting identity for it, and finitely many repairs: either x has odd denominator, or the chosen set is infinite and the repair sum is rational. Finite sums of the terms 1/(2^m - 1) have odd denominators, which is where the denominator enters.
