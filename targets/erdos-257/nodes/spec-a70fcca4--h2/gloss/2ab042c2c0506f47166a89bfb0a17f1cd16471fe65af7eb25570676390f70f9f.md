$$\sum_{a\in S} \frac{1}{2^a-1} \ne \frac12$ for every finite $S \subseteq \mathbb{N}$$

A finite sum of reciprocals of odd numbers is a rational with odd denominator (the term at a = 0 is Lean's 0), and 1/2 is not.
