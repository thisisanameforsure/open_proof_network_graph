$$\exists\, x,y:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}:\quad 0<x_0,\quad 0<y_0,\quad \forall j:\ \ x_j\mid x_{j+1},\quad y_j\mid y_{j+1},\quad 3x_j\le x_{j+1},\quad 3y_j\le y_{j+1}.$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $x_j=3^j$ and $y_j=4^j$: both start at $1$, each power divides the next, and $3\cdot 3^j=3^{j+1}$ while $3\cdot 4^j\le 4^{j+1}$.
