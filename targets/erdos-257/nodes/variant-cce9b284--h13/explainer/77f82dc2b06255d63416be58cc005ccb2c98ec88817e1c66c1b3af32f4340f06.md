## Statement

For $j \ge 1$ and a finite set $A$ of positive integers, $\sum_{a \in A} \frac{1}{2^a - 1} \ne 2^{-j}$. The `tsum` over the finite set is first rewritten as a `Finset` sum over `A.toFinset`.

## Clearing denominators

Put $D = \prod_{a \in A} (2^a - 1)$. Each $2^a - 1$ is odd for $a \ge 1$ (`opn_mersenne_odd`), so $D$ is odd (`opn_prod_odd`). Each $2^a - 1$ divides $D$, so $D \sum_{a\in A} \frac{1}{2^a-1} = N$ with $N = \sum_{a \in A} D/(2^a-1)$ a natural number; the cast lemma `opn_cast_mersenne` matches the natural-number $2^a - 1$ (truncated subtraction, exact here since $2^a \ge 1$) with the real one.

## Parity

If the sum were $2^{-j}$, then $D \cdot 2^{-j} = N$, so $D = 2^j N$ is even because $j \ge 1$. That contradicts $D$ odd. $\blacksquare$

The same parity argument rules out any rational with even denominator as a finite sum of Mersenne reciprocals.
