label: pairwise coprime A with Σ 1/a < ∞ (Erdős 1968)

$$\forall\, A \subseteq \mathbb{N} \text{ infinite, pairwise coprime, with } \sum_{n\in A}\frac1n < \infty:\qquad \sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{2^n-1} \ \text{ is irrational}.$$

"Pairwise coprime" is Mathlib's `Set.Pairwise Nat.Coprime`: $\gcd(m,n) = 1$ for distinct $m, n \in A$. The hypothesis $\sum_{n\in A} \frac1n < \infty$ is Lean's `Summable` of $n \mapsto \frac1n$ on $A$ (with $\frac10 = 0$). Sums are Lean's `tsum` over the set, the ordinary sum of a convergent nonnegative series; the term at $n = 0$ is Lean's $\frac{1}{2^0-1} = \frac10 = 0$.
