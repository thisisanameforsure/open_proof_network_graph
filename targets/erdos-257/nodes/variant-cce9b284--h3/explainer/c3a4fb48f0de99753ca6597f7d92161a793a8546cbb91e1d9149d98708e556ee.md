## Statement

Write $t_k = \frac{1}{2^k-1}$. Let $r$ be the greedy walk of $x = p/2^L$ (index $k \ge 1$ is *chosen* when $t_k \le r_k$, and then $r_{k+1} = r_k - t_k$). Let $L \le n < m$ with $m + 10 \le 2n$, and suppose the walk *stalls* at $n+1$: $\sum_{k \ge n+2} t_k < r_{n+1} < t_{n+1}$. Then $W_n(m) = \sum_{i \ge 0} 2^{-(i+1)} \#\{a \le n \text{ chosen} : a \mid m+i+1\} > 99/100$.

## Binary digits of a Mersenne reciprocal (`h3_F0`, `h3_rec`, `h3_int`)

For $a \ge 1$, $t_a = \sum_{i \ge 0} 2^{-(i+1)} [a \mid i+1]$: the binary expansion of $1/(2^a-1)$ has a $1$ exactly at the positions that are multiples of $a$. Shifting by $m$ places, $2^m t_a - \sum_{i\ge0} 2^{-(i+1)}[a \mid m+i+1]$ is an integer, by induction on $m$: each doubling moves one digit, $[a \mid m+1]$, across the binary point.

## Summing over the chosen set (`h3_walk_sum`, `h3_assembly`)

Let $B$ be the chosen indices up to $n$. Then $r_{n+1} = x - \sum_{a\in B} t_a$, and summing the previous step over $B$ gives $2^m \sum_{a \in B} t_a - W_n(m) \in \mathbb{Z}$. Since $L \le m$, $2^m x$ is an integer. So $J := 2^m r_{n+1} + W_n(m)$ is an integer.

## The stall pins the remainder (`h3_tail`, `h3_upper`)

Write $m = n + k + 1$, so $k + 11 \le n$. The tail satisfies $\sum_{k' \ge n+2} t_{k'} \ge 2^{-(n+1)}$, so $2^m r_{n+1} > 2^k$. And $2^m t_{n+1} = 2^k \cdot \frac{2^{n+1}}{2^{n+1}-1} \le 2^k + \frac{1}{100}$, because $2^{n+1} \ge 4096 \cdot 2^k$. So $2^k < 2^m r_{n+1} < 2^k + \frac{1}{100}$.

## Conclusion

$J = 2^m r_{n+1} + W_n(m) > 2^k$ since $W_n(m) \ge 0$, so the integer $J$ is at least $2^k + 1$, and $W_n(m) = J - 2^m r_{n+1} > 2^k + 1 - 2^k - \frac{1}{100} = \frac{99}{100}$. $\blacksquare$
