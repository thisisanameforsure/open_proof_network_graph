$$\text{stall at } n+1,\ x=p/2^L,\ L\le n<m,\ m+10\le 2n \;\Rightarrow\; W_n(m)>\tfrac{99}{100}$$

W_n(m) weights by 2^-(i+1) the divisors a <= n of m+i+1 chosen by the greedy walk of x.
