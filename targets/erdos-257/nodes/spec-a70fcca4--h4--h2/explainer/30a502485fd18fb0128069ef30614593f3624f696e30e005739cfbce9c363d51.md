## What the assembly shows

The greedy walk of $\frac12$ skips infinitely often, provided the gap bound of `spec-a70fcca4--h4--h1` holds: at every skipped step $m$, $r_m\le 2^{-m}+\frac{1}{3\cdot4^m}$. Here $t_n=\frac{1}{2^n-1}$ (in Lean $t_0=0$), $r_0=\frac12$, $r_{n+1}=r_n-t_n$ when $t_n\le r_n$ and $r_{n+1}=r_n$ otherwise, and $T_{k+1}=\sum_{n>k}t_n$.

## The gap bound {steps: hgap}

The one hole, restating `spec-a70fcca4--h4--h1`. It becomes a lemma of this node.

## Every tail is irrational {steps: h_EB h_tail_irr}

$E=\sum_{n\ge1}t_n$ is irrational by Erdős 1948, proved on this graph as `variant-2f5236aa` and imported. Since $t_n\le 2\cdot2^{-n}$ the series is summable, and $\sum_{i\ge0}t_{M+i}=E-\sum_{i<M}t_i$ is an irrational minus a rational.

## The gap bound lies below the tail {steps: h_skipgap}

Termwise $t_n\ge 2^{-n}+4^{-n}$ for $n\ge1$ (with $x=2^n$: $\frac1x+\frac1{x^2}\le\frac1{x-1}$), so $T_{k+1}\ge\sum_{n>k}(2^{-n}+4^{-n})=2^{-k}+\frac{4^{-k}}3$. Hence at a skip $r_k\le T_{k+1}$.

## The contradiction {steps: hrat hr1 hskip1 hafter hpart hbound hle heq}

Every $r_n$ is rational. Step 1 is skipped ($r_1=\frac12<1=t_1$). Suppose no skip after $m$; let $k\le m$ be the last skip. Every later step is taken, so $\sum_{i<K}t_{k+1+i}=r_{k+1}-r_{k+1+K}\le r_{k+1}=r_k$, giving $T_{k+1}\le r_k$. With the previous section $T_{k+1}=r_k$, a rational tail, against the irrationality of every tail.
