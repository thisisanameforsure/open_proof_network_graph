$$\forall m,\ \exists n,\ m < n \wedge r_n < \frac{1}{2^n-1}$, for the greedy remainder $r$ of $\frac12$$

In words: the set the walk chooses is not co-finite. A skipped step after m rules out a stall at or before m, which is what the assembly below proves.
