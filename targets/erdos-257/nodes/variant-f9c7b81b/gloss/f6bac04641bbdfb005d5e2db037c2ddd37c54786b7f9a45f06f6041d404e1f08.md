$$\lambda\left(\left\{\, \sum_{n \in A} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \;:\; A \subseteq \mathbb{N} \,\right\}\right) = 1$$

where $\lambda$ is Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}$ (Lean's `volume`, valued in $[0,\infty]$) and $A$ ranges over all subsets of $\mathbb{N} = \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$, finite or infinite.

Lean conventions: the sum is `tsum` over the elements of $A$; it is a genuine convergent sum for every $A$, since its terms are at most $2^{1-n}$. At $n = 0$ the divisor $2^0 - 1$ is $0$ and Lean's $1/0$ is $0$, so $A$ and $A \setminus \{0\}$ give the same sum: the set is the set of subset sums of $\{1/(2^n-1) : n \ge 1\}$.
