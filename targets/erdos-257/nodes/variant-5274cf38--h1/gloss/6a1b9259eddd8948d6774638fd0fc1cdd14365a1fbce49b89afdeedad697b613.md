$$\forall B \subseteq \mathbb{N},\ \bigl(B \neq \emptyset \wedge \exists p \ge 1\ \forall n,\ (n \in B \iff n + p \in B)\bigr) \Rightarrow \sum_{n \in B} \frac{1}{2^n - 1} \notin \mathbb{Q}$$

This is Luca–Tachiya's Theorem 1.1 for the 0/1 sequence a(n) = 1_B(n) at q = 2 (an element 0 of B contributes 1/0 = 0 in Lean).
