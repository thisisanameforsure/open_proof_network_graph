$$\exists\, B\subseteq\mathbb{N}:\quad B\ne\emptyset,\qquad \exists\, p\in\mathbb{N}:\ \ 0<p\quad\text{and}\quad \forall n:\ (n\in B\iff n+p\in B).$$

These are the hypotheses of the statement, so the witness shows they can be met. It takes $B=\mathbb{N}$, the whole set of natural numbers, with period $p=1$: the set is nonempty, and every $n$ and every $n+1$ belong to it.
