This file states the theorem `erdos_1050__h1_v2__h4`, which the gate wrote from the hole `hrem` of a merged partial proof.

The statement is about three functions $Q_c:\mathbb N\times\mathbb N\to\mathbb Q$, $Q_x:\mathbb N\to\mathbb Q$ and $A_q:\mathbb N\to\mathbb Q$. It takes as hypotheses that they are given by the following formulas, so these are the only functions it speaks about:

- $Q_c(n,k)=(-1)^k\,2^{k(k-1)/2}\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}\frac{2^{n-i}-1}{2^{i+1}-1}\;\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}\frac{2^{2n-k-i}-1}{2^{i+1}-1}$;
- $Q_x(n)=\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}Q_c(n,k)\,\Big(\frac{3}{2^n}\Big)^k$;
- $A_q(n)=\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\Big(\sum_{j=0}^{k}\frac{Q_c(n,j)}{2^{k-j}-1}\Big)\Big(\frac{3}{2^n}\Big)^k+Q_x(n)\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\frac{3}{2^{j+1}-3}$.

Exponents are natural numbers: subtraction in them is truncated at $0$, and $k(k-1)/2$ is natural-number division (with $k-1=0$ when $k=0$). Where these functions are used below, $k\le n$, so no other exponent is truncated. Division is Lean's division of rationals, in which $x/0=0$: the term $j=k$ of the inner sum in $A_q$ has denominator $2^0-1=0$ and so contributes $0$.

The statement has one further hypothesis: for every $n$ there is a natural number $d>0$ with $d^2\le 2^{3n^2}$ such that $d\,Q_x(n)$ and $d\,A_q(n)$ are integers. This is word for word the conclusion of the node `erdos-1050--h1-v2--h3`. The conclusion below does not mention $d$.

Let $T=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2^{m+3}-3}$, a real number (Lean's `tsum` over all $m\in\mathbb N$, which would be $0$ for a series that does not converge; this one converges). For each $n$ write, in the real numbers,
$$R_n=3T\,Q_x(n)-A_q(n).$$

The conclusion: for every natural number $n$,

- $R_n>0$, and
- $R_n^2\cdot 2^{4n^2}\le 2^{2n+4}$, that is, $R_n\le 2^{\,n+2-2n^2}$.
