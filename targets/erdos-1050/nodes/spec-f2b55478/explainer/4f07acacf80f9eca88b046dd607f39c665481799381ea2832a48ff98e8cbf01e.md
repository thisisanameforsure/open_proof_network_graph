## Overview

Write $c_j$ for the $j$-th numerator on the left, $c_j = (-1)^j 2^{\lfloor j(j-1)/2\rfloor}\prod_{i<j}\frac{2^{n-i}-1}{2^{i+1}-1}\prod_{i<n}\frac{2^{2n-j-i}-1}{2^{i+1}-1}$, and put
$$N(x) = \prod_{i=0}^{n-1}\bigl(x - 2^{\,n+1+i}\bigr), \qquad D(x) = \prod_{k=0}^{n}\bigl(x - 2^{k}\bigr).$$
The proof shows that both sides of the theorem equal $N(2^m)/D(2^m)$. The left side is rewritten as $\sum_j c_j 2^j/(2^m-2^j)$ (`hl`), which is the partial-fraction expansion of $N(x)/D(x)$ at $x = 2^m$ (`hpf`, resting on the residue identity `hres`); the right side is rewritten directly as $N(2^m)/D(2^m)$ (`hr`). The last two lines are `rw [hl, hr]; exact hpf`. Code comments label `hres`, `hpf`, `hl`, `hr` as steps 4, 3, 1, 2 of the annex fcdb7a3a on this node.

## The residue identity {steps: hres hres.s1 hres.E1test hres.E1test.s1 hres.E1test.s2 hres.E2test hres.E3test hres.E4test hres.hQ hres.hQ.this hres.hA hres.hA.this hres.F1 hres.F2 hres.F3 hres.hp hres.hp.h2 hres.hp.s2 hres.hp.s3 hres.hQj hres.hQr hres.hAj}

`hres`: for each $j \le n$,
$$c_j \cdot 2^j \cdot \prod_{k \le n,\, k \ne j}(2^j - 2^k) = N(2^j).$$
Write $n = j + r$ (`hres.s1`). Four product evaluations do the work. `hres.E1test` splits the product over $k \ne j$ into $k<j$ and $k = j+1+x$ with $x<r$ (by induction on $r$: `hres.E1test.s1`, `hres.E1test.s2`). `hres.E2test`: $\prod_{k<j}(2^j-2^k) = 2^{j(j-1)/2}\prod_{i<j}(2^{i+1}-1)$. `hres.E3test`: $\prod_{x<r}(2^j-2^{j+1+x}) = (-1)^r 2^{jr}\prod_{i<r}(2^{i+1}-1)$. `hres.E4test`: $N(2^j) = (-1)^{j+r}2^{j(j+r)}\prod_{i<j+r}(2^{r+1+i}-1)$. Then `hres.F1` and `hres.F2` reverse the order of the two numerator products of $c_j$ so that both become products of $2^{r+1+i}-1$, and `hres.F3` splits $\prod_{i<j+r}(2^{i+1}-1)$ into its first $r$ and last $j$ factors. `hres.hp` collects the powers of two: $2\lfloor j(j-1)/2\rfloor + j + jr = j(j+r)$, using that $j(j-1)$ is even (`hres.hp.h2`; cases $j=0$, `hres.hp.s2`, and $j \ge 1$, `hres.hp.s3`). With every denominator product nonzero (`hres.hQ`, `hres.hA` and their instances `hres.hQj`, `hres.hQr`, `hres.hAj`), `field_simp` closes the identity.

## Partial fractions by Lagrange interpolation {steps: hpf hpf.hinj hpf.hdeg hpf.key hpf.hev hpf.hj' hpf.hxj hpf.hxj.this hpf.hE1 hpf.hE1.hk' hpf.hE1.this hpf.hE2}

`hpf`: $\sum_{j=0}^{n} c_j 2^j/(2^m-2^j) = N(2^m)/D(2^m)$. The nodes $2^0,\dots,2^n$ are distinct (`hpf.hinj`), and $N$ has degree $n$, less than the number of nodes (`hpf.hdeg`), so $N$ equals its own Lagrange interpolant on those nodes (`hpf.key`, Mathlib's `Lagrange.eq_interpolate_of_eval_eq`). Evaluating both at $x = 2^m$ (`hpf.hev`) writes $N(2^m)$ as $\sum_j N(2^j)\prod_{k\ne j}\frac{2^m-2^k}{2^j-2^k}$. Dividing by $D(2^m) = (2^m-2^j)\prod_{k \ne j}(2^m-2^k)$ term by term, and substituting `hres` for $N(2^j)$, gives the left side. The divisions are legitimate because $j < m$ (`hpf.hj'`), so $2^m \ne 2^j$ (`hpf.hxj`), and the products $\prod_{k\ne j}(2^m-2^k)$ and $\prod_{k \ne j}(2^j - 2^k)$ are nonzero (`hpf.hE1`, `hpf.hE2`).

## Rewriting the left side {steps: hl hl.hj' hl.h2 hl.hp}

`hl`: each term $c_j/(2^{m-j}-1)$ equals $c_j 2^j/(2^m-2^j)$. Since $j \le n < m$ (`hl.hj'`), $2^m = 2^{m-j}2^j$ (`hl.h2`), so $2^m - 2^j = (2^{m-j}-1)2^j$, and $2^j \ne 0$ (`hl.hp`) can be cancelled.

## Rewriting the right side {steps: hr hr.hB hr.hB.this hr.key hr.s3 hr.s3.hA hr.s3.hA.e hr.s3.hD hr.s3.hD.e hr.s3.hs hr.s3.hDX hr.s3.hDX.this hr.s4 hr.s4.hz1 hr.s4.hz2}

`hr`: the right side equals $N(2^m)/D(2^m)$. First the factor $\prod_{i<n}(2^{i+1}-1)$, which is nonzero (`hr.hB`), cancels against the denominators inside the middle product, leaving $2^{n\cdot n}\prod_{i<n}(2^{m-n-1-i}-1)\big/\prod_{j\le n}(2^{m-j}-1)$ (`hr.key`). Then two cases.

If $m > 2n$ (`hr.s3`): no exponent truncates, and factoring out powers of two gives $\prod_{i<n}(2^{m-n-1-i}-1) = N(2^m)/2^{\sum_{i<n}(n+1+i)}$ (`hr.s3.hA`, from $2^m = 2^{m-n-1-i}2^{n+1+i}$, `hr.s3.hA.e`) and $\prod_{j \le n}(2^{m-j}-1) = D(2^m)/2^{\sum_{j\le n} j}$ (`hr.s3.hD`, from `hr.s3.hD.e`). The exponents differ by exactly $n\cdot n$ (`hr.s3.hs`: $\sum_{i<n}(n+1+i) = n\cdot n + \sum_{j\le n} j$), which cancels $2^{n\cdot n}$; with $D(2^m) \ne 0$ (`hr.s3.hDX`), `field_simp` finishes.

If $m \le 2n$ (`hr.s4`): the index $i = m-n-1$ lies below $n$, its factor $2^{m-n-1-i}-1$ is $2^0-1 = 0$ (`hr.s4.hz1`), and the same index makes the factor $2^m - 2^{n+1+i}$ of $N(2^m)$ zero (`hr.s4.hz2`), so both expressions are $0$.
