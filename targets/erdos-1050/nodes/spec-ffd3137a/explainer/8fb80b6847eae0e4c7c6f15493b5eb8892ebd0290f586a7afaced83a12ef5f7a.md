## Overview

The proof is a partial-fraction computation evaluated at a pole. Take the nodes $x_l = 2^l$ ($0 \le l \le n$), the points $y_i = 2^{n+1+i}$ ($0 \le i < n$), and weights $w_j = Q_c(n,j)\,2^j$. Three general facts are proved first, then specialised: a residue identity for the weights (`lag_test`), the partial-fraction expansion of $\prod_i (X - y_i)$ over the nodes (`pf_test`), and its derivative at one node (`pf2_test`). The specialisation (`hpf`) gives
$$\sum_{j \ne k}\frac{Q_c(n,j)2^j}{2^k - 2^j} = Q_c(n,k)\,2^k\Bigl(\sum_{i<n}\frac{1}{2^k - 2^{n+1+i}} - \sum_{l \ne k}\frac{1}{2^k-2^l}\Bigr),$$
and steps `T0` to `T3` rewrite each piece into the form of the statement. The final line is `linear_combination hpf - T1 + T2 - T3`, after `T0`.

## The residue identity {steps: lag_test lag_test.hpos lag_test.hpos.this lag_test.hpos.this.s1 lag_test.hpos.this.s2 lag_test.hb lag_test.hb.h1 lag_test.hb.h1.hl' lag_test.hb.h1.this lag_test.hb.hs' lag_test.hc lag_test.hc.s1 lag_test.hc.s2 lag_test.hd lag_test.hd.s1 lag_test.hd.s2 lag_test.he lag_test.he.hn lag_test.he.ht' lag_test.hf lag_test.hf.hi' lag_test.hF lag_test.hB lag_test.hjj lag_test.hjj.h2 lag_test.hjj.s2 lag_test.hjj.s3 lag_test.hpow}

`lag_test`: for $j \le n$, $Q_c(n,j)\cdot 2^j\cdot\prod_{l<j}(2^j-2^l)\cdot\prod_{s<n-j}(2^j-2^{j+1+s}) = \prod_{i<n}(2^j - 2^{n+1+i})$, with $Q_c(n,j)$ written out. Each product is evaluated: $\prod_{l<j}(2^j-2^l) = 2^{j(j-1)/2}\prod_{s<j}(2^{s+1}-1)$ (`lag_test.hb`, factoring $2^j - 2^l = 2^l(2^{j-l}-1)$, `lag_test.hb.h1`); $\prod_{s<n-j}(2^j - 2^{j+1+s}) = (-2^j)^{n-j}\prod_{s<n-j}(2^{s+1}-1)$ (`lag_test.hc`); $\prod_{i<n}(2^j-2^{n+1+i}) = (-2^j)^n\prod_{i<n}(2^{n+1+i-j}-1)$ (`lag_test.hd`). `lag_test.he` merges $\prod_{i<j}(2^{n-i}-1)$ and $\prod_{s<n-j}(2^{s+1}-1)$ into $\prod_{s<n}(2^{s+1}-1)$; `lag_test.hf` reindexes $\prod_{i<n}(2^{2n-j-i}-1)$ as $\prod_{i<n}(2^{n+1+i-j}-1)$. The sign and power bookkeeping is `lag_test.hjj` ($j\cdot j = 2\lfloor j(j-1)/2\rfloor + j$) and `lag_test.hpow` ($(-2^j)^n$ split into the factors that appear). With the denominators nonzero (`lag_test.hpos`, `lag_test.hF`, `lag_test.hB`), `field_simp` and `ring` close it.

## Partial fractions over the nodes {steps: pf_test pf_test.hL pf_test.hE pf_test.s3 pf_test.s3.s1 pf_test.s3.s2 pf_test.s4 pf_test.s4.s1 pf_test.s4.s2 pf_test.s4.s3 pf_test.s4.s4}

`pf_test`, for arbitrary rational $x$, $y$, $w$ with the $x_0,\dots,x_n$ distinct: if $w_j\prod_{l\ne j}(x_j - x_l) = \prod_{i<n}(x_j - y_i)$ for every $j \le n$, then, as polynomials, $\sum_{j\le n} w_j\prod_{l \ne j}(X - x_l) = \prod_{i<n}(X - y_i)$. Both sides have degree at most $n$ (`pf_test.hL`, `pf_test.hE`; the degree bound is `pf_test.s3`), so it is enough that they agree at the $n+1$ distinct points $x_j$ (`pf_test.s4`). At $X = x_j$ only the $j$-th summand on the left survives, the others containing the factor $x_j - x_j = 0$, and the hypothesis is exactly the equality there.

## The derivative at a node {steps: pf2_test pf2_test.hLE pf2_test.hne pf2_test.hP0 pf2_test.hd pf2_test.h1 pf2_test.h1.hjs pf2_test.h1.hjk pf2_test.h1.hks pf2_test.h1.s4 pf2_test.h1.s4.this pf2_test.h1.s5 pf2_test.h1.s6 pf2_test.h2 pf2_test.h2.this pf2_test.h3 pf2_test.h3.this pf2_test.h1'}

`pf2_test`: under the same hypotheses, and with $x_k \ne y_i$ for all $i$,
$$\sum_{j \ne k}\frac{w_j}{x_k - x_j} = w_k\Bigl(\sum_{i<n}\frac{1}{x_k-y_i} - \sum_{l\ne k}\frac{1}{x_k - x_l}\Bigr).$$
It differentiates the polynomial identity of `pf_test` (`pf2_test.hLE`) and evaluates at $x_k$ (`pf2_test.hd`). Let $P = \prod_{l\ne k}(x_k - x_l)$, nonzero by distinctness (`pf2_test.hne`, `pf2_test.hP0`). On the left, a summand with $j \ne k$ contributes $w_j P/(x_k - x_j)$ (`pf2_test.h1`, summed in `pf2_test.h1'`), and the summand $j = k$ contributes $w_k P\sum_{m\ne k}1/(x_k-x_m)$ (`pf2_test.h2`). On the right the derivative of $\prod_i(X - y_i)$ at $x_k$ is $\prod_i(x_k-y_i)\sum_i 1/(x_k-y_i)$, which is $w_k P \sum_i 1/(x_k-y_i)$ by the hypothesis at $j=k$ (`pf2_test.h3`). Cancelling $P$ gives the identity.

## Specialising to the nodes 2^l {steps: hc hk1 h2 hs1 hs1.this hs1.this.s1 hs1.this.s2 hs1' hinj hinj.this hx hxy hxy.this hw hw.hjn hw.e1 hw.e2 hw.e3 hw.e3.ga hw.e3.ga.this hw.e3.gb hpf hpk}

`hc` unfolds $Q_c$. Then the hypotheses of `pf2_test` are checked for $x_l = 2^l$, $y_i = 2^{n+1+i}$, $w_j = Q_c(n,j)2^j$: the nodes are distinct because $l \mapsto 2^l$ is injective (`hinj`, `hx`); $2^k \ne 2^{n+1+i}$ because $k \le n$ (`hxy`); and the weight condition `hw` is `lag_test` after splitting $\prod_{l \ne j}(2^j - 2^l)$ into $l<j$ and $l = j+1+s$ (`hw.e1` to `hw.e3`). `hpf` is the result, displayed in the overview. Supporting facts: $k \le n$ (`hk1`), powers of two are nonzero (`h2`), $2^{b+1}-1 \ne 0$ and $1-2^{b+1} \ne 0$ (`hs1`, `hs1'`), and $2^{k-j}-1 \ne 0$ for $j<k$ (`hpk`).

## Matching the statement {steps: T0 T1 T1.e1 T1.ga T1.ga.hjk T1.gb T1.gb.this T2 T2.e T2.this T3a T3a.e1 T3a.ha T3a.ha.this T3a.hb T3b T3b.e1 T3b.e1.hlk T3b.this T3c T3c.this T3}

`T0`: the term $j = k$ of the statement's sum divides by $2^0-1 = 0$ and is $0$, so the sum is over $j<k$. `T1`: the left side of `hpf` splits into $j<k$, where $\frac{Q_c(n,j)2^j}{2^k-2^j} = \frac{Q_c(n,j)}{2^{k-j}-1}$ (`T1.ga`), and $j = k+1+b$ with $b < n-k$, where it equals $-\frac{Q_c(n,k+b+1)2^{b+1}}{2^{b+1}-1}$ (`T1.gb`). `T2`: $Q_c(n,k)2^k\sum_i \frac{1}{2^k-2^{n+1+i}} = Q_c(n,k)\sum_i\frac{1}{1-2^{n+1+i-k}}$. `T3a` splits $\sum_{l\ne k}\frac{1}{2^k-2^l}$ into $l<k$ and $l = k+1+b$; `T3b` gives $2^k\sum_{l<k}\frac{1}{2^k-2^l} = \sum_{b<k}\frac{2^{b+1}}{2^{b+1}-1}$ (each term is $\frac{2^{k-l}}{2^{k-l}-1}$, `T3b.e1`, then reindexed); `T3c` gives $2^k\sum_{b<n-k}\frac{1}{2^k-2^{k+1+b}} = \sum_{b<n-k}\frac{1}{1-2^{b+1}}$; `T3` combines them. Substituting `T1`, `T2`, `T3` into `hpf` and rearranging is the statement.
