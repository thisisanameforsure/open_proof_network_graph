Borwein's integrality route: transfer T to the reduced q-harmonic value z = 3/5 − 3T, then b(n)z − a(n) = 9^n W(n) E(n) with integers a, b and a cleared error that tends to 0.

The one hole is `integrality`: integer sequences a, b with b(n)·z − a(n) = 9^n W(n) E(n) for every n ≥ 1. The assembly proves the transfer (`transfer`), the non-vanishing of W(n) (`w_ne`) and of E(n) (`e_ne`, every term of the series has the sign (−1)^(n−1)), and the decay of 9^n W(n) E(n) (`decay`), then reads the approximants to T off as 5a(n+1) − 3b(n+1) and −15 b(n+1).
