The real number
$$T=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{2^{n+3}-3}=\frac15+\frac1{13}+\frac1{29}+\frac1{61}+\cdots$$
is irrational: it is not equal to any rational number.

The statement has no hypotheses. The sum is Lean's unconditional sum `∑'` over all natural numbers $n \ge 0$, computed in the real numbers. Lean gives such a sum the value $0$ when the series is not summable; the statement does not assert summability separately.
