$$\forall k\ge3,\ \forall A\subseteq[1,6k+5],\ [6k+2,6k+5]\subseteq A\ \land\ |A|=4k+4\ \Rightarrow\ \forall\text{ odd }l\in[5,2k+1],\ \exists\text{ an injective coprime cyclic ordering of length }l\text{ in }A$$

Consecutive entries, including the last and first, must be coprime.
