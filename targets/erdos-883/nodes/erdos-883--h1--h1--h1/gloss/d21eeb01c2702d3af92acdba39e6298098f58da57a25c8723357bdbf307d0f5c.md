$$\forall n\ge18,\ \forall A\subseteq[1,n],\ |A|>\lfloor n/2\rfloor+\lfloor n/3\rfloor-\lfloor n/6\rfloor\ \Rightarrow\ \forall\text{ odd }l\in[5,\lfloor n/3\rfloor+1],\ \exists\text{ an injective coprime cyclic ordering of length }l\text{ in }A$$

Consecutive entries, including the last and first, must be coprime.
