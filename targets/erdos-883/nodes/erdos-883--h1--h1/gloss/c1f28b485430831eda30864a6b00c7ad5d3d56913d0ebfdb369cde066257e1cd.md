$$|A|>\lfloor n/2\rfloor+\lfloor n/3\rfloor-\lfloor n/6\rfloor,\ l\text{ odd},\ 5\le l\le\lfloor n/3\rfloor+1\implies\exists\text{ distinct }a_0,\dots,a_{l-1}\in A\text{ with }\gcd(a_i,a_{(i+1)\bmod l})=1$$
