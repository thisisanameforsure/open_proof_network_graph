$$\exists\, n, A, l:\quad A\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\}\ \wedge\ \left\lfloor \tfrac n2\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor \tfrac n3\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor \tfrac n6\right\rfloor<|A|\ \wedge\ l\ \text{odd}\ \wedge\ 3\le l\le\left\lfloor \tfrac n3\right\rfloor+1$$

The witness is $n=6$, $A=\{1,\dots,6\}$, $l=3$: $|A|=6>3+2-1=4$, $l=3$ is odd, and $3\le 6/3+1=3$.

**Lean conventions.** `/` is floor division on ℕ and `-` is truncated subtraction; here $6/2+6/3-6/6=4$ with no truncation. `Odd l` is witnessed by $3=2\cdot1+1$.

The witness shows the hypotheses of the statement can all hold at once, so the theorem is not vacuous. It says nothing about the conclusion: it does not exhibit a triangle in $G(\{1,\dots,6\})$ (one is $1,2,3$).
