label: Odd cycles in dense coprime graphs
---
$$\forall n\in\mathbb N,\ \forall A\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\}:\quad |A| > \left\lfloor \tfrac n2\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor \tfrac n3\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor \tfrac n6\right\rfloor \implies \forall\, l \text{ odd},\ 3\le l\le \left\lfloor \tfrac n3\right\rfloor+1:\ G(A)\ \text{contains a cycle of length } l,$$
where $G(A)$ is the graph on vertex set $A$ in which distinct $a,b\in A$ are adjacent iff $\gcd(a,b)=1$.

**Lean conventions.**
- `n / 2`, `n / 3`, `n / 6` are natural-number divisions, i.e. floors, exactly the $\lfloor\cdot\rfloor$ of the problem.
- The `-` is truncated subtraction on ℕ, but it never truncates here: $\lfloor n/6\rfloor\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor$.
- `l ≤ n / 3 + 1` is $l\le\lfloor n/3\rfloor+1$, which for an integer $l$ is the same as the problem's $l\le n/3+1$.
- `coprimeGraph` is `SimpleGraph.fromRel Nat.Coprime`: adjacency is $a\ne b$ and $\gcd(a,b)=1$ (no loops, so $1$ is adjacent to every other element but not to itself). It is induced on the subtype of `A`.
- `oddCycleLengths` is the set of odd $m$ such that some closed walk of length $m$ is a cycle in Mathlib's sense (`Walk.IsCycle`: no repeated vertices, length $\ge 3$).

The bound $\lfloor n/2\rfloor+\lfloor n/3\rfloor-\lfloor n/6\rfloor$ is the number of integers in $\{1,\dots,n\}$ divisible by $2$ or $3$. That set has no triangle in $G(A)$, since among any three of its elements two are even or two are multiples of $3$, so the threshold is already sharp for $l=3$. The statement says one element beyond that extremal size forces every short odd cycle at once; Erdős and Sárközy (1997) studied it.
