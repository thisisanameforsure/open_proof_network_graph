$$2 \le k,\ 2(k+1) \le n-2 \Rightarrow \binom{n}{k} \ne \binom{n-2}{k+1}$$

Known: Stroeker and de Weger, Acta Arith. 87 (1999) 339–365, solve the equation C(n,k) = C(n−2,k+1) completely; the case (m,l) = (n−2,k+1) of Theorem 2.2 of Blokhuis, Brouwer, de Weger (2017). Not formalised.
