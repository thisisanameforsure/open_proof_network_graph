$$\exists K, B \in \mathbb{N}\ \ \forall a \in \mathbb{N}:\quad \#\Bigl\{\, n \;:\; \exists k,\ K < k,\ k < e^{(\log n)^{5/6}},\ 2k \le n,\ \tbinom{n}{k} = a \,\Bigr\} \le B$$

$\log$ is the natural logarithm of $n$ as a real number, and $k < e^{(\log n)^{5/6}}$ is compared in $\mathbb{R}$. The set collects **rows** $n$, each counted once, with `Set.ncard` as the count.

In words: past some fixed $K$, and below the threshold $e^{(\log n)^{5/6}}$, each number appears in a bounded number of rows of the half triangle, the bound $B$ not depending on $a$. Bounded $k$ is the sibling hole `--h1`, and $k \ge e^{(\log n)^{5/6}}$ is `--h3`.
