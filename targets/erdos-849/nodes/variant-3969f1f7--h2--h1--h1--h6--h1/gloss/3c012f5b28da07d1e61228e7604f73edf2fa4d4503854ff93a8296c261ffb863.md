$$\forall x, y \in \mathbb{Z},\ y^2 + y = x^3 - x \Rightarrow (x, y) \in \{(-1,0), (-1,-1), (0,0), (0,-1), (1,0), (1,-1), (2,2), (2,-3), (6,14), (6,-15)\}$$

The ten integral points of the elliptic curve y^2 + y = x^3 - x (LMFDB 37.a1, Cremona 37a1, rank 1), as listed by the LMFDB; Mordell (1963) determined the integer solutions of y(y+1) = x(x+1)(x+2), the same curve.
