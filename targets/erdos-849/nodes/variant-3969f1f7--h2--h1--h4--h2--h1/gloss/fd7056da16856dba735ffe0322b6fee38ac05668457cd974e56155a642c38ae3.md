$$2 \le k,\ 2(k+2) \le n-1 \Rightarrow \binom{n}{k} \ne \binom{n-1}{k+2}$$

Known: Stroeker and de Weger, Acta Arith. 87 (1999) 339–365, solve the equation C(n,k) = C(n−1,k+2) completely; stated as the case (m,l) = (n−1,k+2) of Theorem 2.2 of Blokhuis, Brouwer, de Weger, Integers 17 (2017) #A64 ("no unknown collisions"; none is known of this shape). Not formalised.
