$$X, Y \in \mathbb{Z},\ 60\,Y(Y-1) = X(X-1)(X-2)(X-3)(X-4) \implies (X,Y) \in \{(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1),(2,0),(2,1),(3,0),(3,1),(4,0),(4,1),(5,-1),(5,2),(6,-3),(6,4),(7,-6),(7,7),(15,-77),(15,78),(19,-152),(19,153)\}$$

Y. Bugeaud, M. Mignotte, S. Siksek, M. Stoll, Sz. Tengely, Integral points on hyperelliptic curves, Algebra & Number Theory 2 (2008) 859–885, Theorem 2: the integral solutions of Y(Y−1)/2 = X(X−1)(X−2)(X−3)(X−4)/120, here multiplied by 120.
