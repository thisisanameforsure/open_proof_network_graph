$$\exists a_0 \in \mathbb{N}\ \ \forall a \ge a_0:\quad \#\Bigl\{\, n \;:\; \exists k,\ 1 \le k,\ e^{(\log n)^{5/6}} \le k,\ 2k \le n,\ \tbinom{n}{k} = a \,\Bigr\} \le 2$$

$\log$ is the natural logarithm of $n$ as a real number and the threshold is compared in $\mathbb{R}$; $1 \le k$ is implied by the threshold, since $e^{x} \ge 1$ for $x \ge 0$. The set collects **rows** $n$, each counted once, with `Set.ncard` as the count.

In words: every sufficiently large $a$ appears in at most two rows $n$ at some $k$ with $e^{(\log n)^{5/6}} \le k \le n/2$.
