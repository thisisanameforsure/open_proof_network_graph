$$\exists a_0\ \forall a \ge a_0:\ \#\{n : \binom{n}{k} = a,\ e^{(\log n)^{5/6}} \le k \le n/2\} \le 2$$

Known: Matomäki, Radziwiłł, Shao, Tao, Teräväinen, Quart. J. Math. 73 (2022), Theorem 1.3, with ε = 1/6: for a large, at most two solutions of C(n, k) = a with exp((log n)^(2/3+ε)) ≤ k ≤ n/2.
