$$\binom{n}{4} \ne \binom{m}{6}\ \text{ for all }\ n \ge 8,\ m \ge 12$$

R. J. Stroeker and B. M. M. de Weger, Math. Comp. 68 (1999) 1257–1281: C(n,4) = C(m,6) solved completely; no half-triangle solution. Via BBdW 2017 Thm 2.2.
