$$\forall x, y \in \mathbb{Z},\ y^2 + y = x^3 + x^2 - 58x + 1294 \Rightarrow (x, y) \in \{(-13,4), (-13,-5), (-7,37), (-7,-38), (-2,37), (-2,-38), (2,34), (2,-35), (8,37), (8,-38), (14,58), (14,-59), (23,112), (23,-113), (68,562), (68,-563), (133,1537), (133,-1538), (233,3562), (233,-3563), (323,5812), (323,-5813), (2234,105613), (2234,-105614)\}$$

The twenty-four integral points of the elliptic curve $y^2 + y = x^3 + x^2 - 58x + 1294$ (LMFDB 14475.a1, Cremona 14475f1, rank 2), as listed by the LMFDB. With $w = m(m-5)$, $720 C(m,6) = w(w+4)(w+6)$, and $360 n(n-1) = w(w+4)(w+6)$ is this curve after scaling by 6 and translating.
