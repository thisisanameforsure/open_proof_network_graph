$$\binom{n}{2} = \binom{m}{5},\ n \ge 4,\ m \ge 10 \Rightarrow (n,m) \in \{(78,15), (153,19)\}$$

Y. Bugeaud, M. Mignotte, S. Siksek, M. Stoll, Sz. Tengely, Integral points on hyperelliptic curves, Algebra & Number Theory 2 (2008) 859–885, Theorem 2: all integer solutions of C(Y,2) = C(X,5) are the trivial ones with X ≤ 7 and (X,Y) = (15,78), (19,153) (and the mirrored Y ↦ 1−Y); checked against the arXiv text (0801.4459). Via BBdW 2017 Thm 2.2.
