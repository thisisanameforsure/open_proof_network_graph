$$\binom{n}{k} = \binom{m}{l},\ 2 \le k \le n/2,\ 2 \le l \le m/2,\ k < l,\ (k,l) \in \{(2,3),(2,4),(2,5),(2,6),(2,8),(3,4),(3,6),(4,6),(4,8)\} \Rightarrow (n,k,m,l) \in \{(78,2,15,5), (78,2,14,6), (16,2,10,3), (21,2,10,4), (56,2,22,3), (120,2,36,3), (153,2,19,5), (221,2,17,8)\}$$

Known: for each of these pairs the collision equation is a curve of positive genus solved completely: (2,3) Avanesov, Acta Arith. 12 (1966) 409–420; (2,4) Pintér, Publ. Math. Debrecen 47 (1995) 411–415 and de Weger, Quart. J. Math. Oxford (2) 47 (1996) 221–231; (2,5) Bugeaud, Mignotte, Siksek, Stoll, Tengely, Algebra Number Theory 2 (2008) 859–885; (2,6), (2,8), (3,6), (4,6), (4,8) Stroeker and de Weger, Math. Comp. 68 (1999) 1257–1281; (3,4) Mordell, Pacific J. Math. 13 (1963) 1347–1351. Collected as Theorem 2.2 of Blokhuis, Brouwer, de Weger, Integers 17 (2017) #A64.
