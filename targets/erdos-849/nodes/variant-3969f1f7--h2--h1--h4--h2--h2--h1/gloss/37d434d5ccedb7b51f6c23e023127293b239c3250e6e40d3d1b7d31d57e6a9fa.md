$$u, v \in \mathbb{Z},\ u^3 - 3u^2v + 4uv^2 - v^3 + 3u^2 - 7uv + 4v^2 + 2u - 3v = 0 \implies (u,v) \in \{(-2,0),(-1,-1),(-1,0),(-1,1),(0,0),(0,1),(0,3),(1,1),(1,6)\}$$

R. J. Stroeker and B. M. M. de Weger, Solving elliptic Diophantine equations: the general cubic case, Acta Arith. 87 (1999) 339–365, §7.1, Theorem 4 (equation (40)). With u = k and v = n, (40) is n(n−1)(k+1) − (n−k)(n−k−1)(n−k−2) = 0, the equation C(n,k) = C(n−2,k+1) with denominators cleared; (1,6) is C(6,1) = C(4,2) = 6.
