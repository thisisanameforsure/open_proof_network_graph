$$\exists C\,\exists P_0\,\forall P\ge P_0:\ \text{for }(n,k,n',k')\text{ as in Lemma 3.1 obeying (i), (ii)},\ N,M\in\{k,n,n-k,k',n',n'-k'\},\ 1\le j\le(\log P)^{1/2},\ c=\mathbb{E}_p\{\tfrac{N}{p}\}\{\tfrac{M}{p^j}\}-\mathbb{E}_p\{\tfrac{N}{p}\}\,\mathbb{E}_p\{\tfrac{M}{p^j}\}\ \text{over primes }p\in[\lceil P\rceil,\lfloor P+\tfrac{P}{(\log P)^{100}}\rfloor]:\ \text{(i) }j\ge2\Rightarrow|c|\le\tfrac{C}{(\log P)^{10}};\ \text{(ii) }j=1,\ \min(|N|,|M|)\le\tfrac{P}{(\log P)^{1000}}\Rightarrow|c|\le\tfrac{C}{(\log P)^{1000}};\ \text{(iii) }j=1,\ |N|,|M|>\tfrac{P}{(\log P)^{1000}},\ a\perp b,\ 1\le a,b\le(\log P)^{1/100},\ |aN-bM|\le\tfrac{P}{(\log P)^{1000}}\Rightarrow\bigl|c-\tfrac{1}{12ab}\bigr|\le\tfrac{C}{(\log P)^{1/1000}};\ \text{(iv) }j=1,\ |N|,|M|>\tfrac{P}{(\log P)^{1000}},\ \text{no such }a,b\Rightarrow|c|\le\tfrac{C}{(\log P)^{1/1000}}$$

Matomäki, Radziwiłł, Shao, Tao, Teräväinen (2022), Proposition 3.2, proved in §3 from the equidistribution estimate Proposition 1.12 (§4) by a truncated Fourier expansion of 1/2 − {x}. The expectation is the average over that finite set of primes; ≪ is the constant C.
