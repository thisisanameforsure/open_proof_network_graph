$$\binom{n}{2} = \binom{m}{4},\ n \ge 4,\ m \ge 8 \Rightarrow (n,m) = (21,10)$$

Á. Pintér, A note on the Diophantine equation C(x,4) = C(y,2), Publ. Math. Debrecen 47 (1995) 411–415, and B. M. M. de Weger, A binomial Diophantine equation, Quart. J. Math. Oxford (2) 47 (1996) 221–231: the only nontrivial solution is 210 = C(21,2) = C(10,4). Via BBdW 2017 Thm 2.2.
