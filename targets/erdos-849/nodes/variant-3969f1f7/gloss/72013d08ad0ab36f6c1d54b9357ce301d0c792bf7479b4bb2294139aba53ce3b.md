$$\Bigl(\exists B\ \forall a:\ \#R(a) \le B\Bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; \neg\Bigl(\forall t \ge 1\ \exists a:\ \#R(a) = t\Bigr), \qquad R(a) = \bigl\{n : \exists k \ge 1,\ 2k \le n,\ \tbinom{n}{k} = a\bigr\}$$

The counts are `Set.ncard` of sets of rows, as in both statements. If every count is at most $B$, the root's instance $t = B + 1$ asks for an $a$ with $\#R(a) = B + 1 > B$, which cannot exist.
