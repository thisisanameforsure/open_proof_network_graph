label: Singmaster's conjecture, row form
---
$$\exists B \in \mathbb{N}\ \ \forall a \in \mathbb{N}:\quad \#\bigl\{\, n \in \mathbb{N} \;:\; \exists k \ge 1,\ 2k \le n,\ \tbinom{n}{k} = a \,\bigr\} \le B$$

Here $B$, $a$, $n$, $k$ are natural numbers and $2k \le n$ is $1 \le k \le n/2$. The set collects **rows** $n$ (each counted once), and its size is `Set.ncard`, which is $0$ for an infinite set; every such set is finite and lies in $[2, a]$ (statement `spec-38faff03`), so here it is the true count.

In words: there is one bound $B$ such that no number appears in more than $B$ rows of the half triangle $1 \le k \le n/2$. This is Singmaster's conjecture in the row form of the root, and with the relation below it says the root is false.
