$$u, v \in \mathbb{Z},\ 15u^3 - 15u = v^3 - 4v^2 + 3v \implies (u,v) \in \{-1,0,1\} \times \{0,1,3\} \cup \{(2,6),(8,21)\}$$

R. J. Stroeker and B. M. M. de Weger, Solving elliptic Diophantine equations: the general cubic case, Acta Arith. 87 (1999) 339–365, §7.1 (first example): the equation comes from C(n,3) = C(m,6), and its integral solutions are the eleven listed, as proved in their Elliptic binomial Diophantine equations, Math. Comp. 68 (1999) 1257–1281, Theorem B36.
