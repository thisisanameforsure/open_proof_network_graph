$$\binom{n}{2} = \binom{m}{3},\ n \ge 4,\ m \ge 6 \Rightarrow (n,m) \in \{(16,10), (56,22), (120,36)\}$$

È. T. Avanesov, Solution of a problem on figurate numbers, Acta Arith. 12 (1966/67) 409–420: all integer solutions of x(x−1)/2 = y(y−1)(y−2)/6; restricted here to the half triangle. Collected as Theorem 2.2 (pairs) of Blokhuis–Brouwer–de Weger, Integers 17 (2017) #A64.
