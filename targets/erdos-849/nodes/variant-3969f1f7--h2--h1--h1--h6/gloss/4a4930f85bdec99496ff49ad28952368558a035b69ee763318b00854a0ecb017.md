$$\binom{n}{3} \ne \binom{m}{4}\ \text{ for all }\ n \ge 6,\ m \ge 8$$

L. J. Mordell, On the integer solutions of y(y+1) = x(x+1)(x+2), Pacific J. Math. 13 (1963) 1347–1351: the equivalent equation solved completely (so attributed by BBdW 2017, proof of Thm 2.2); no half-triangle collision.
