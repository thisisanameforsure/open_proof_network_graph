$$u, v \in \mathbb{Z},\ u^3 - 2u^2v + 3uv^2 - v^3 + 3u^2 - 3uv + 3v^2 + 2u = 0 \implies (u,v) \in \{(-2,-2),(-2,-1),(-2,0),(-1,-1),(-1,0),(-1,1),(0,0),(0,3)\}$$

R. J. Stroeker and B. M. M. de Weger, Solving elliptic Diophantine equations: the general cubic case, Acta Arith. 87 (1999) 339–365, §7.1, Theorem 3 (equation (39)). With u = k and v = n, (39) is n(k+1)(k+2) − (n−k)(n−k−1)(n−k−2) = 0, the equation C(n,k) = C(n−1,k+2) with denominators cleared.
