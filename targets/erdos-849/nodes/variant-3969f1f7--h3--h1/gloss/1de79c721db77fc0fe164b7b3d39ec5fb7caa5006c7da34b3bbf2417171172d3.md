$$\exists a_1\ \forall a \ge a_1$: any two solutions $(n,k), (n',k')$ of $\binom{n}{k} = a$ with $e^{(\log n)^{5/6}} \le k \le n/2$ satisfy $|n-n'| + |k-k'| \le \exp\big((\log\log a)^{9/10}\big)$$

Matomäki, Radziwiłł, Shao, Tao, Teräväinen, Quart. J. Math. 73 (2022), §1.2: Proposition 1.9 (distance estimate) combined with the size bounds (1.5), (1.7) gives m − m', n' − n ≪ exp(O((log log t)^{1−ε'})) for every ε' < ε/(2/3+ε); here ε = 1/6, ε' = 3/20, and the O and the ≪ are absorbed by a₁.
