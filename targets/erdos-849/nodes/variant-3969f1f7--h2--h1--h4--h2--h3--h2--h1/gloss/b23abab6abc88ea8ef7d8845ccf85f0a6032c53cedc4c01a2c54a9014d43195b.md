$$x \ge 25 \implies \exists\, p \text{ prime},\ x < p < 6x/5$$

J. Nagura, On the interval containing at least one prime number, Proc. Japan Acad. 28 (1952) 177–181: for every x ≥ 25 there is a prime p with x < p < 6x/5 (here 5p < 6x, in ℕ).
