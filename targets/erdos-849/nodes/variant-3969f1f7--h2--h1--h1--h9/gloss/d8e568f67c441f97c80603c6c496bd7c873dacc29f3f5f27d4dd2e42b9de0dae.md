$$\forall n, m \in \mathbb{N}:\quad 8 \le n \to 16 \le m \to \tbinom{n}{4} \ne \tbinom{m}{8}$$

Here $n$, $m$ are natural numbers; the bounds $8 \le n$ and $16 \le m$ are the half-triangle conditions $2 \cdot 4 \le n$ and $2 \cdot 8 \le m$ for the fixed columns $k = 4$, $l = 8$. The inequation is the conclusion, not a hypothesis.

In words: no number is both $\binom{n}{4}$ and $\binom{m}{8}$ with $(n, 4)$ and $(m, 8)$ in the half triangle: the collision equation for the column pair $(4, 8)$ has no solutions there.
