$$\forall x, y \in \mathbb{Z},\ y^2 + y = x^3 - 9x + 20 \Rightarrow (x, y) \in \{(-3,4), (-3,-5), (-2,5), (-2,-6), (0,4), (0,-5), (1,3), (1,-4), (3,4), (3,-5), (6,13), (6,-14), (10,30), (10,-31), (12,40), (12,-41), (27,139), (27,-140), (63,499), (63,-500), (105,1075), (105,-1076)\}$$

The twenty-two integral points of the elliptic curve y^2 + y = x^3 - 9x + 20 (LMFDB 1611.a1, Cremona 1611d1, conductor 3^2 * 179, rank 2), as listed by the LMFDB. Its short model is y^2 = x^3 - 144x + 1296.
