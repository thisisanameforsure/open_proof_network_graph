label: distance estimate for two solutions (MRSTT Prop 1.9)

$$\exists C \ge 0\ \ \exists a_1 \in \mathbb{N}\ \ \forall a \ge a_1\ \ \forall n, k, n', k' \in \mathbb{N}:\quad \Bigl(1 \le k,\ 2k \le n,\ \tbinom{n}{k} = a\Bigr) \to \Bigl(1 \le k',\ 2k' \le n',\ \tbinom{n'}{k'} = a\Bigr) \to \Bigl[\ |k - k'| \le C\, e^{(\log(n + n'))^{43/60}}\ \ \wedge\ \ \Bigl(e^{(\log(n + n'))^{43/60}} \le k \to e^{(\log(n + n'))^{43/60}} \le k' \to |n - n'| \le C\, e^{(\log(n + n'))^{43/60}}\Bigr)\ \Bigr]$$

Here $C$ is a real number and $a_1$, $a$, $n$, $k$, $n'$, $k'$ are natural numbers; $\log$ is the natural logarithm of the sum cast to $\mathbb{R}$, the powers are real powers, and the absolute values and the comparisons with $k$ and $k'$ are in $\mathbb{R}$ after casting. The two solutions are not required to be distinct, and no interior condition is placed on them beyond the half-triangle range $2k \le n$, $2k' \le n'$.

In words: there are a constant $C$ and a threshold $a_1$ such that, for $a \ge a_1$, any two solutions $(n, k)$, $(n', k')$ of $\binom{n}{k} = a$ in the half triangle have columns at most $C e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$ apart, and, when both columns are at least $e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$, rows at most that far apart as well. The exponent $43/60$ is $2/3 + \varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon = 1/20$.
