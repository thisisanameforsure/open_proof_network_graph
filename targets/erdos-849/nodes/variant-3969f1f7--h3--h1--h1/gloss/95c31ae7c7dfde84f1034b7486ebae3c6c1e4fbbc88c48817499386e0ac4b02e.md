$$\exists C \ge 0\ \exists a_1\ \forall a \ge a_1$: two solutions $(n,k),(n',k')$ of $\binom{n}{k}=a$ in the left half satisfy $|k-k'| \le C e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$, and $|n-n'| \le C e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$ when both $k, k' \ge e^{(\log(n+n'))^{43/60}}$$

Matomäki, Radziwiłł, Shao, Tao, Teräväinen, Quart. J. Math. 73 (2022), Proposition 1.9 with ε = 1/20 (2/3 + 1/20 = 43/60); the ≪_ε is the constant C, "t sufficiently large" is a₁. Deep: rests on the equidistribution estimate Proposition 1.12 (their §3, §4).
