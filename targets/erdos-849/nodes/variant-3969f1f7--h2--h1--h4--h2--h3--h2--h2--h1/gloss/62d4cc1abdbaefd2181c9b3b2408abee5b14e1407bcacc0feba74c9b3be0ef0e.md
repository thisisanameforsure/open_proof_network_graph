$$x \ge 2010760 \implies \exists\, p \text{ prime},\ x < p < x + x/16597$$

L. Schoenfeld, Sharper bounds for the Chebyshev functions θ(x) and ψ(x). II, Math. Comp. 30 (1976) 337–360 (as quoted by Dusart, arXiv:1002.0442, §6.1.3): for x ≥ 2010759.9 the open interval (x, x + x/16597) contains a prime. Here in ℕ: 16597 p < 16598 x.
