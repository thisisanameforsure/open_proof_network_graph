$$\binom{n}{k} = \binom{n-1}{k+1}$ with $2 \le k \le n/2,\ 2(k+1) \le n-1 \Rightarrow (n,k) = (F_{2i+2}F_{2i+3},\ F_{2i}F_{2i+3})$ for some $i \ge 1$$

Known: Tovey, Fibonacci Quart. 23 (1985) 356–358 (the equation reduces to a Pell equation in Q(√5)); the family is Lind's, Fibonacci Quart. 6 (1968) 86–93, rediscovered by Singmaster, Fibonacci Quart. 13 (1975) 295–298. Stated as the case (m,l) = (n−1,k+1) of Theorem 2.2 of Blokhuis, Brouwer, de Weger, Integers 17 (2017) #A64.
