$$\#\{(n,k) : 2 \le k,\ 2k \le n,\ \binom{n}{k} = t\} \le C \Rightarrow N(t) \le 2C + 2$$

The edges k = 1 and k = n - 1 give only n = t; every other solution is in the left half or is the mirror image (n, n - k) of one there.
