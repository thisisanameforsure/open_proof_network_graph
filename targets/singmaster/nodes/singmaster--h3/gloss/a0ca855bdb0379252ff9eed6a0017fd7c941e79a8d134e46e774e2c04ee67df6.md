$$\exists C\ \forall t,\ \#\{(n,k) : 2 \le k,\ 2k \le n,\ \binom{n}{k} = t\} \le C$$

Singmaster's conjecture in its normalised form: only binomial coefficients with 2 ≤ k ≤ n/2 are counted.
