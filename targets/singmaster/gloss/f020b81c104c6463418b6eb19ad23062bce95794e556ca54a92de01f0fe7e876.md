$$\mathrm{solutions}(t) = \{(n,k) \in \mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N} : 1 \le k,\ k < n,\ \tbinom{n}{k} = t\}$$

For a natural number $t$, the set of positions $(n,k)$ of Pascal's triangle, row $n$ and column $k$, at which the entry $\binom{n}{k}$ equals $t$, leaving out the two border columns $k = 0$ and $k = n$ (where every entry is 1). Rows and columns are natural numbers, and $\binom{n}{k}$ is Mathlib's Nat.choose.
