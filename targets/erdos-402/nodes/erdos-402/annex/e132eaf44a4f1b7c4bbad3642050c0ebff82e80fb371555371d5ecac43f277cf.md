Informal argument behind the skeleton of Graham's gcd conjecture (Erdős problem 402) filed as the partial `attempts/20260917T141927Z-calib-402-f480-partial.lean` (graph PR #79).

Statement. For a finite set A of positive integers there are a, b in A with gcd(a, b) <= a/|A|; equivalently max over a, b in A of a/gcd(a, b) is at least |A|. Conjectured by Graham (1970); proved for all sufficiently large |A| by Szegedy (Combinatorica 6, 1986) and Zaharescu (1987), with an explicit threshold by Cheng and Pomerance (Rocky Mountain J. Math. 24, 1994); proved for all finite sets by Balasubramanian and Soundararajan, "On a conjecture of R. L. Graham", Acta Arith. 75 (1996), 1-38, which also settles Graham's characterisation of the equality cases {1, ..., n}, {L/1, ..., L/n} with L = lcm(1..n), and {2, 3, 4, 6}.

Architecture shared by the published proofs, as the three holes of the skeleton state it:

1. Farey structure (hole h1). If every ratio a/gcd(a, b) over A is at most m (written without division as a <= m * gcd(a, b)), then for a, b in A the quotient a/b is a reduced fraction u/v with 1 <= u, v <= m: take d = gcd(a, b), u = a/d, v = b/d; then u and v are coprime, a * v = b * u, and the bounds are the hypothesis applied to (a, b) and to (b, a). With m = |A| - 1 this is the reformulation that a counterexample would be a set of |A| numbers all of whose pairwise quotients are Farey fractions of order |A| - 1.

2. Reduction to primitive sets (hole h2). The ratio a/gcd(a, b) is invariant under multiplying the whole set by a constant, so if the conclusion holds for every set of |A| positive integers with gcd 1, it holds for A: divide A by g = gcd(A); the image has the same size (division by g is injective on multiples of g), gcd 1, and for a' = a/g, b' = b/g one has gcd(a, b) = g * gcd(a', b') while a/|A| = g * a'/|A|, so the inequality transfers.

3. The core (hole h3). A set with gcd 1 all of whose quotients are Farey fractions of order |A| satisfies the bound. This is where the published arguments live: with M the largest element, every a in A has a/M = u/v with v | M, so A sits inside {M u / v : v | M, v <= |A|, u <= v coprime to v}; Szegedy and Zaharescu show that for |A| large a prime in a suitable range must divide M and its "dual" L/M (L = lcm(A)) and derive a contradiction from size, which needs |A| large through Chebyshev-type prime estimates; Balasubramanian and Soundararajan replace the prime by a sieve-produced integer and settle every |A|, and handle the small sets that remain.

Assembly (proved in the partial). Reduce to a primitive B of the same size by (2). If the bound failed on B, every a in B would satisfy a < |B| * gcd(a, b) for every b in B, so a <= |B| * gcd(a, b), which by (1) with m = |B| gives the Farey structure of order |B|; then (3) gives the bound on B after all, a contradiction.

Every hole is stated positively with satisfiable hypotheses ({1} satisfies each), because a hole becomes a child node that needs a non-vacuity witness before anyone can work on it; a "suppose the theorem fails" lemma could never be witnessed. Holes are ordered so that each later hole inherits only lemmas easier than itself.
