The file proves an auxiliary theorem, `exists_prime_divisor`. It states that for every natural number $m$ with $2 \le m$ there is a $p$ such that `Opn.IsPrime p` and `Opn.Divides p m`. These are the graph's own notions of primality and divisibility, taken from an import whose gloss is not given here. Informally: every natural number $m \ge 2$ has a prime divisor $p$.

The theorem `witness` asserts that there exist natural numbers $n, d, m$ satisfying four conditions together:

- For every natural number $k$ with $0 < k$ and $k \le n$, $k$ divides $n!$ (in the sense of `Opn.Divides`).
- For every natural number $m$ with $2 \le m$ there is a $p$ with `Opn.IsPrime p` and `Opn.Divides p m`. Here $m$ is a fresh universally quantified variable, separate from the existentially chosen $m$.
- $d$ divides $m$.
- $d$ divides $m + 1$.

The file exhibits $n = 0$, $d = 1$, $m = 1$. The first condition holds vacuously, since no $k$ satisfies both $0 < k$ and $k \le 0$. The second condition is discharged by `exists_prime_divisor`. The last two say that $1$ divides $1$ and $1$ divides $2$.
