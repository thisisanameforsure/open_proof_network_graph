The theorem `infinitude_of_primes__h2` asserts the following. For every natural number $n$, suppose every natural number $k$ with $0 < k$ and $k \le n$ divides $n!$, where "divides" is `Opn.Divides`. Under that hypothesis, for every natural number $m$ with $2 \le m$ there exists some $p$ such that $p$ is prime and $p$ divides $m$. Here "prime" is `Opn.IsPrime` and "divides" is again `Opn.Divides`.

The hypothesis about $n$ does not mention $m$ or $p$, so the conclusion does not depend on $n$. The file does not write the type of $p$; it is whatever argument type `Opn.IsPrime` and `Opn.Divides` take. No gloss of either definition from `Defs.IsPrime` was provided, so their meanings are taken from their names only. The proof in the file is `sorry`.
