For all natural numbers $n$ and $k$: if $0 < k$ and $k \le n$, then $k$ divides $n!$.

Divisibility here is the problem's own definition `Opn.Divides`: $k$ divides $m$ when $m = k \cdot c$ for some natural number $c$.
